Why We Built ICT Forex Academy

Hamara udesy yahi hai ki hum logo ko sahi direction me lekar jayein. Yahan aapko koi fake lifestyle ya rent ki car dikhakar course nahi becha jayega, balki asli institutional knowledge di jayegi.

Trading me 6 Month se 1 Year Dena Kyun Zaroori Hai?

Agar aap kisi bhi field me kuch naya sikhne jaate hain, toh wahan bhi 2 se 3 saal lag jaate hain. Trading ek aisi cheez hai jo aasaani se nahi seekhi ja sakti. Jo log bina sike trade karte hain, woh hamesha loss karte hain.

Lekin agar aap is site ko rozana **30 minutes se 1 hour** dete hain aur lagatar **6 months se 1 year** tak dedicated practice karte hain, toh aapki trading pehle se kahin zyada behtar ho jayegi. Market ko dekhne ka aapka tarika bilkul badal jayega, aap apne trading rules ko strictly follow karne lagenge, aur dheere-dheere apni mistakes ko bhi kam kar denge. Jab aap apne losses ko control karna sikh jaate hain, tabhi aap ek profitable trader ban paate hain.

Instagram aur Social Media ke "Fake Influencers" se Saavdhan

Aajkal market me fake influencers bohot badh gaye hain. Woh Instagram par badi-badi cars (jo rent par li hoti hain), Dubai ka lifestyle aur "maine aaj itna kama liya" ke screenshots dikhakar apna mehanga course bechte hain. Woh sirf apna profit dikhate hain kyunki unhe apna course bechna hota hai; woh kabhi apna loss nahi dikhayenge kyunki asal me trading se zyada paisa wo courses bech kar hi kama rahe hain.

Aise logo ke chakkar me padkar naye log apna poora capital wipe out kar dete hain. Jo insaan aapko sirf course bechne ke liye aage aaye, use turant avoid karein. Hamesha sahi mentor ko chune jo transparency me vishwas rakhta ho.
