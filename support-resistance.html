Mastering Support & Resistance

Support & Resistance ise hum (stock market, crypto, forex) is sabhi me ham use karke Trade plan kar sakte hai. Ye ek technical analysis ke sabse important aur basic concepts hain. Inki madad se traders ye pata lagate hain ki market mein price kahan tak gir sakta hai aur kahan tak jaakar ruk ya palat sakta hai.

1. Support Kya Hai? (Floor)

Support wo price level ya zone hota hai jahan par stock ya asset ka price girte-girte ruk jata hai, kyunki wahan par buying pressure (kharidar) zyada ho jati hai.

Kaise kaam karta hai: Jab price neeche aata hai aur support zone par pahunchta hai, toh log use sasta samajhkar kharidne lagte hain, jisse price wapas upar ki taraf bounce karne lagta hai. Yeh ek tarah se "floor" (farsh) ki tarah kaam karta hai jiske neeche price asani se nahi girta.

2. Resistance Kya Hai? (Ceiling)

Resistance wo price level ya zone hota hai jahan par price badhte-badhte ruk jata hai, kyunki wahan par selling pressure (bikhar) zyada ho jati hai.

Kaise kaam karta hai: Jab price upar jata hai aur resistance level par pahunchta hai, toh traders apni holdings bechne lagte hain (profit book karne ke liye), jisse price upar jaane se ruk jata hai aur wapas neeche aane lagta hai. Yeh ek "roof" (chhat) ki tarah kaam karta hai.

Inka Use Kaise Kiya Jata Hai?

  • Trading Entries: Aam taur par traders Support ke paas stock kharidte hain (Buy) aur Resistance ke paas aane par bech dete hain (Sell).
  • Breakout & Breakdown: Agar price resistance ko todkar uske upar nikal jaye, toh ise Breakout kehte hain aur iske baad price tezi se upar ja sakta hai. Waisa hi agar support toot jaye (Breakdown), toh price aur neeche gir sakta hai.
  • Role Reversal: Ek baar jab koi resistance toot jata hai, toh wahi purana resistance baad mein Support ban jata hai (aur purana support tootne par resistance ban jata hai).

Live Chart Par Analysis

Chalo ham ise live chart pe dekhte hai, taki aap acche se samaj sako ise chart pe kaise lagana hai. Niche diye gaye image ko dekho.

Ye hamara live chart hai isme aap dekh sakte hai ham live chart me ise kaise laga sakte hai (drow kar sakte hai). Uper wala drow hai jo line khichi dikh rahi hai use ham resistence kahte hai. Yani wo level jaha sellers bethe hai sell ke liye, aur niche wala hai wo support hai, vaha pe buyers hai. Jaise hi price us area pe aati hai, ya to breakout aata hai ya to reversal aata hai. Jab buyers aur sellers me ladai hoti hai to price usi range me ghumti hai Support aur resistance ke bich me.

Jaise aap image me dekh rahe hai uske baad, price ne Resistance ko toda hai, waha pe breakout aaya hai. Aur market me aur buyers aa gaye jo market ko upar le gaye yaha seller low ho gaye isliye breakout aaya hai. Us jagah buyers ne sellers ko hara diya.

Trade Plan Kaise Karein? (Risk-Reward Ratio)

Abhi hum dusre image me dekhte hai. Ham iske madad se trade kaise plan kar sakte hai, ise samajhte hai.

Aap is image me dekh pa rahe hai, isme hamne entry plan ki hai, yaha ek price ne zone tayar kiya usme hi price ghum rahi thi, uske baad ek breakout diya, wo fail ho gaya wapas se us zone me aa gaya uske baad usi resistance ko touch kiya waha pe price ruk gayi iska matlab waha abhi seller mojud hai, to hamne waha sell entry ka plan kiya aur hame risk reward RR 1:4 ka mila hai yani loss hoga $1 aur profit hoga $4 ka yani hamara 4 guna zyada ka profit.

Agar hamne 10 trade me se 6 trade loss kar de aur sirf 4 trade profit kare to bhi ham profitable hai. Aise hi market se hame profit nikalna hota hai. Ye hamne sirf support & resistance sikha hai, abhi candlestick pattern, price action, ICT, SMC, Liquidity, ye sab mila liya to ham beginners se advance level ki strategy banana sikh jayenge.
