📊 Module 1: Price Levels

Mastering Support & Resistance

Sikhein ki market me Support aur Resistance level kaise kaam karte hain, kahan buyers aur sellers active hote hain, aur inka use karke perfect entry kaise banayein.

Jab price Support par aata hai toh buyers khareedte hain (Bounce Up), aur Resistance par pahunchkar sellers bechte hain (Bounce Down).

1. Support Kya Hai? (Floor)

Definition: Support woh price level hai jahan market ke girne ki raftar tham jaati hai kyunki yahan **Buyers (Demand)** bahut zyada active ho jaate hain.

Kaise Kaam Karta Hai: Jaise hi price support level ko touch karta hai, achanak khareedari (buying pressure) shuru ho jaati hai aur price wapas upar ki taraf bounce karne lagta hai.

Trading Tip: Support level ke paas hamesha **BUY** ka mauka dekha jata hai.

2. Resistance Kya Hai? (Ceiling)

Definition: Resistance woh price level hai jahan market ke upar jaane ki limit khatam ho jaati hai kyunki yahan **Sellers (Supply)** bahut zyada active hote hain.

Kaise Kaam Karta Hai: Jab price badhte-badhte resistance level par pahunchta hai, toh log profit booking karte hain ya nayi bikri (selling) shuru hoti hai, jisse price neeche girne lagta hai.

Trading Tip: Resistance level ke paas **SELL** ya profit book karne ka sign milta hai.

Breakout aur Breakdown Kya Hota Hai?

Hamesha market support ya resistance se bounce nahi karta. Kai baar volume zyada hone par yeh levels toot (break) bhi jaate hain:

🚀 Resistance Breakout

Jab price resistance ko todkar upar nikal jata hai, toh iska matlab hai buyers ki jeet ho gayi hai aur market aur tezi se upar jayega (Strong Buy).

🔻 Support Breakdown

Jab price support level ko todkar neeche gir jata hai, toh sellers haavi ho jaate hain aur market aur tezi se neeche girta hai (Strong Sell).
