Support & Resistance

The single most important concept in trading. Every professional trader — from retail to institutional — uses Support & Resistance as their foundation. Master this chapter and you will see the market differently forever.

1

What is Support & Resistance?

Support and Resistance are price zones where the market repeatedly reacts. They exist because large institutions (banks, hedge funds) place their buy/sell orders at specific price levels — creating strong demand (support) or supply (resistance).

SUPPORT

A price zone below current price. Buyers are waiting here. When price falls to this zone, buyers push price back UP.

Floor of the Market ↑

RESISTANCE

A price zone above current price. Sellers are waiting here. When price rises to this zone, sellers push price back DOWN.

Ceiling of the Market ↓
📊 Chart — Support & Resistance Zones

Price bounces 3 times from Support zone and rejects 2 times from Resistance zone

Remember: S&R are ZONES, not exact lines. Price can slightly overshoot before reversing. Always treat them as areas of 5-20 pips wide.

2

How to Identify S&R on a Chart

Follow these 4 steps to mark S&R levels like a professional trader:

1

Switch to Higher Timeframe

Always start marking S&R on Daily or H4 chart first. Higher timeframe levels are more powerful and respected by institutions.

2

Find Swing Highs & Swing Lows

Look for price peaks (swing highs = resistance) and price valleys (swing lows = support). These are natural turning points in the market.

3

Mark Levels with Multiple Touches

A level is valid only if price has touched and reversed at least 2-3 times. Single touches are weak. Look for confluences.

4

Drop to Lower Timeframe for Entry

Once level is marked on H4/Daily, switch to H1 or M15 to find precise entry signals.

📊 Chart — Swing Highs & Swing Lows

Connect Swing Highs → Resistance Line  |  Connect Swing Lows → Support Line

3

Types of S&R Levels

1. Horizontal S&R

MOST POWERFUL

Flat price levels where market reversed multiple times. More touches = stronger level.

2. Role Reversal (S&R Flip)

HIGH PROBABILITY

When Resistance is broken, it becomes Support. When Support breaks, it becomes Resistance. This creates excellent entry opportunities.

3. Round Number Levels

Prices like 1.2000, 150.00 act as natural S&R. Strongest when aligned with horizontal S&R.

Entry Strategies Using S&R

STRATEGY 1

Bounce Trade — Buy at Support / Sell at Resistance

Wait for price to reach a known S&R level, look for a rejection candle (pin bar, engulfing), then enter.

1

Mark S&R level on H4 or Daily chart

2

Wait for price to reach that level

3

See rejection candle (pin bar / engulfing)

4

Enter BUY (at support) or SELL (at resistance)

SL

Stop Loss 5-10 pips BEYOND the level

STRATEGY 2

Breakout & Retest — Enter after flip

Wait for price to break through a level with strong momentum, then retest that flipped level before entering.

1

Price breaks above resistance with big candle

2

DO NOT chase — wait for retest of old resistance

3

Old resistance now acts as support → Enter BUY

SL

SL below the flipped level

STRATEGY 3

False Breakout (Trap Trade)

Price appears to break a level but quickly reverses back (liquidity grab). This traps weak traders and creates a strong move in the opposite direction. Enter when price closes back inside the range. Very powerful with ICT concepts.

Price breaks level → immediately reverses → Enter opposite direction with tight SL
5

Risk Management Rules

Stop Loss Rules

Always place SL 5-15 pips beyond the S&R level

Never move SL to breakeven too early

If level breaks clearly → close trade, don't hope

Take Profit Rules

Minimum 1:2 Risk:Reward ratio on every trade

TP at the next major S&R level opposite side

Partial close at 1:1, rest at 1:3

Position Sizing

Risk only 1-2% of account per trade maximum

Calculate lot size based on SL distance

Never increase position to recover losses

Best Trading Times

London Session: 8:00–12:00 GMT

New York Session: 13:00–17:00 GMT

Avoid 30 min before/after major news
