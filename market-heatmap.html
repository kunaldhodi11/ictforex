How to read this page

What the colours and numbers mean

  • Change is the move from the daily open to the latest price, as reported by the data feed. It is not measured from yesterday's close.
  • Green means the market is above its daily open, red means below. A stronger colour means a bigger move. Full colour is reached at about 1% for forex pairs, 2% for Gold and indices, and 5% for Bitcoin.
  • Grey or "N/A" means the feed did not give a value for that market. We never fill gaps with made-up numbers.
  • Currency strength averages the moves of all pairs containing that currency. It helps you see which currencies are leading and lagging, nothing more.
  • Forex, Gold and indices are closed at weekends, so you will see the last prices from the previous session.

Questions

Heatmap FAQ

Is this a trading signal?

No. The heatmap only shows how markets have moved so far today. A green tile is not a recommendation to buy and a red tile is not a recommendation to sell.

Why do prices differ from my broker?

The prices come from a third-party feed and are indicative. They can be delayed, interrupted, incomplete or different from the prices on your broker platform. Always trade from your own broker's prices.

Where does the data come from?

Prices are loaded directly in your browser from the free biquote market data service. This website does not store or resell the prices. Because your browser contacts biquote.io, that service can see your IP address, as with any website you visit.

Why is a market missing or showing N/A?

Some instruments, especially index CFDs such as NAS100 and US30, may not always be offered by the free feed. When that happens the tile shows N/A instead of a guess.
