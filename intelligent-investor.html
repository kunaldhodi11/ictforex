The Intelligent Investor: The Definitive Book on Value Investing

Author: Benjamin Graham

Chapter 1: Investment aur Speculation mein Farq

Ek asli investor aur speculator ke beech ka mukhya antar:

  • Investment: Gahra adhyayan (analysis) karke mool dhan ki suraksha aur uchit return prapt karna.
  • Speculation: Market ki short-term tezi-mandi par bina soche-samjhe jua khelna.

Chapter 2: Investor aur Inflation (Mehangai)

Inflation kis tarah aapke paise ki taqat ko kam karti hai aur usse bachne ke tarike:

  • Bank savings se mehangai ko maat nahi di ja sakti.
  • Long-term mein achhi quality ke equity stocks aur real assets mein nivesh karna aavashyak hai.

Chapter 3: "Mr. Market" ka Concept

Market ke mood swings ko samajhne ke liye Graham ka famous concept:

  • Mr. Market roz aapko shares kharidne ya bechne ke alag-alag daam deta hai.
  • Ek intelligent investor ko Mr. Market ke emotions ke hisab se nahi, balki apni samajh ke anusaar kaam karna chahiye.

Chapter 4: Defensive Investor ki Portfolio Strategy

Jin niveshkon ke paas market ko track karne ka samay nahi hota:

  • Stocks aur Bonds ka santulit portfolio (jaise 50-50 ratio) banana chahiye.
  • Index funds aur diversified large-cap companies mein nivesh karna behtar hota hai.

Chapter 5: Enterprising Investor ki Active Approach

Jo niveshkon ke paas samay hai aur jo research kar sakte hain:

  • Undervalued stocks aur special situations ko dhoondhna.
  • Secondary issues aur bargain-issue companies ka adhyayan karna.

Chapter 6: Margin of Safety (Suraksha ka Ghera)

Poori kitab ka sabse mukhya aur mahatvapurna niyam:

  • Hamesha intrinsic value se kaafi kam daam par stock kharidein.
  • Margin of safety hone par future ki anischitataon se nuksan ki sambhavna kam ho jati hai.

Chapter 7: Portfolio Management aur Psychology

Apne dimaag aur emotions par niyantran rakhna:

  • Bina discipline ke bade returns hasil karna asambhav hai.
  • Market ki ghabrahat (panic) ya lalach (greed) mein aakar apne long-term goals ko na badlein.
