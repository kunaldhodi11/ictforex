Author: Benjamin Graham (Warren Buffett ke Guru)

Chapter 1: Investment aur Speculation mein Farq (Investment vs. Speculation)

Benjamin Graham ke mutabiq ek asli investor aur speculator mein zameen-aasmaan ka farq hota hai:

  • Investment: Jab aap kisi business ka gahra adhyayan (analysis) karke paise lagate hain jisme aapka mool dhan (principal) surakshit rahe aur achha return mile.
  • Speculation: Jab log market ki short-term tezi-mandi ya bhawishya ki ummeed par bina soche-samjhe jua khelte hain. Yeh kafi jokhim bhara hota hai.

Chapter 2: Investor aur Inflation (Mehangai ka Asar)

Inflation (mehangai) aapke paise ki taqat ko dheere-dheere kam kar deti hai:

  • Agar aap apne paise ko sirf bank savings mein rakhte hain, toh mehangai ki wajah se aapki purchasing power kam ho jati hai.
  • Isliye long-term mein achhi quality ke stocks (shares) ya real assets mein invest karna zaroori hai jo inflation ko maat de sakein.

Chapter 3: "Mr. Market" ka Concept (Bazaar ka Mood)

Graham ne market ko samajhane ke liye ek behad shandar character banaya hai jiska naam **Mr. Market** hai:

  • Mr. Market aapka ek aisa business partner hai jo roz aapke paas aata hai aur aapko apne shares kharidne ya bechne ke liye ek naya daam (price) batata hai.
  • Mr. Market ki bhavnayein roz badlati hain—kabhi wo bahut khush aur optimistic hota hai (shares ke daam badha deta hai), aur kabhi wo dar kar panic mein aa jata hai (shares ke daam bahut gira deta hai).
  • Ek intelligent investor wahi hai jo Mr. Market ke mood swings se prabhavit na ho, balki jab wo saste daam par bechne aaye toh kharide aur jab mehnge daam mange toh use nazarandaz kare.

Chapter 4 & 5: Defensive aur Enterprising Investor ki Strategies

Kitab mein niveshkon ko unke samay aur anubhav ke aadhar par do bhago mein baanta gaya hai:

  • Defensive Investor (Passive): Jinke paas market ko track karne ka samay nahi hota. Unhe apne portfolio mein Stocks aur Bonds ka santulan (jaise 50-50) rakhna chahiye aur index funds mein nivesh karna chahiye.
  • Enterprising Investor (Active): Jo samay de sakte hain aur achhi companies ki balance sheet padh sakte hain, wo undervalued stocks aur special situations ko dhoondh kar extra return kama sakte hain.

Chapter 6: Margin of Safety (Suraksha ka Ghera) — Sabse Bada Niyam

Poori kitab ka nichod is ek niyam mein chhipa hai:

  • Margin of Safety ka matlab hai ki kisi bhi stock ya asset ko uski asli value (intrinsic value) se kaafi kam daam par kharidna.
  • Misal ke taur par, agar kisi company ki value 100 rupaye per share hai aur wo market mein 60 rupaye par mil rahi hai, toh aapke paas 40 rupaye ka safety margin hai. Agar future mein kuch galat bhi ho jaye, toh aapka nuksan hone ki sambhavna bahut kam hoti hai.
