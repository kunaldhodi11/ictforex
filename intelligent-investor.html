The Intelligent Investor: The Definitive Book on Value Investing

Benjamin Graham

Benjamin Graham ke 20-chapter framework par based 30-topic Hinglish study guide: investing principles, portfolio policy, security analysis, valuation aur investor discipline.

    Key concept

    Investment vs speculation

    Graham ke framework mein investment ke liye thorough analysis, principal ki safety aur adequate return ka standard important hai.

    • Speculation mein uncertainty aur price movement par bet hoti hai; ise investment samajhkar risk underestimate na karein.
    • Analysis ke bina sirf tips, hype ya recent price rise par buy karna disciplined investing nahi hai.
    • Even careful analysis ke baad bhi loss possible hai.
    Key concept

    Mr. Market: price aur value ko alag samjho

    Mr. Market ek imaginary business partner hai jo roz alag mood mein price offer karta hai. Uska mood aapko control nahi karna chahiye.

    • Market quote ko information aur opportunity ke roop mein dekhein, command ke roop mein nahi.
    • Price intrinsic value se kaafi neeche ho to research ke baad opportunity ho sakti hai; high price par caution zaroori hai.
    • Intrinsic value exact number nahi; estimate range aur assumptions ke saath sochiye.
    Key concept

    Defensive investor approach

    Graham ka defensive (passive) investor woh hai jo serious mistakes se bachna, principal ki safety par dhyan dena aur investment ke liye kam time/effort dena chahta hai.

    • Graham ne high-grade bonds aur diversified, financially sound common stocks ka mix suggest kiya.
    • Book ka traditional starting point 50% stocks aur 50% bonds hai; unhone aam taur par stocks ko 25% se 75% ke beech rakhne ki range discuss ki. Yeh Graham ka historical framework hai, har aaj ke investor ke liye universal prescription nahi.
    • Graham ke approach mein portfolio ko discipline ke saath rebalance karna aur stocks ko excessive price par na kharidna important hai.
    • Broad low-cost index funds modern investors ke liye ek practical adaptation ho sakte hain, lekin ise Graham ke original stock-selection rules ka exact replacement na samjhein.
    Key concept

    Enterprising investor approach

    Graham ka enterprising (active) investor market se behtar result paane ki koshish mein zyada time, effort aur skill lagane ko taiyar hota hai. Sirf zyada risk lena is category ka matlab nahi hai.

    • Company ki financial strength, earnings record, debt, business prospects aur management ko carefully analyse karein.
    • Undervalued opportunities dhoondhne mein research aur patience lagta hai; sirf low P/E ya low share price kaafi nahi.
    • Graham ne active investor ke liye bhi discipline, diversification aur margin of safety ko important maana; concentrated positions ko automatic recommendation na samjhein.
    • Extra effort se better result milna guaranteed nahi hai. Agar investor ke paas time, knowledge ya temperament na ho, defensive approach zyada suitable ho sakti hai.
    Key concept

    Margin of safety

    Estimated intrinsic value aur purchase price ke beech gap uncertainty ke liye buffer deta hai.

    • Valuation assumptions galat ho sakti hain; margin of safety estimation error ko absorb karne mein madad karta hai.
    • Business quality, balance sheet, cash flow aur industry risk ko valuation ke saath dekhein.
    • Margin of safety risk ko reduce kar sakta hai, eliminate nahi.
    Key concept

    Investor psychology aur discipline

    Long-term results mein temperament aur process ka role bahut bada hai.

    • Fear aur greed ke dauran pre-written investment rules follow karein.
    • Past performance ko future guarantee na samjhein.
    • Regular review karein, lekin daily price fluctuations par impulsive decisions na lein.
    Study topic 7 of 30

    Inflation aur purchasing power

    Inflation cash ki real buying power ko kam kar sakti hai. Graham ne investors ko nominal return ke saath purchasing power par bhi sochne ko kaha.

    • Nominal interest ya dividend ko real return samajhne ki galti na karein.
    • Stocks inflation se automatic protection nahi dete; pricing power, costs aur valuation matter karte hain.
    • Portfolio ko goals, time horizon aur risk capacity ke hisaab se diversify karein.
    Study topic 8 of 30

    Stock-market history aur expectations

    Graham historical market data ko context ke liye dekhte hain, future predict karne ke liye nahi.

    • Past bull ya bear market future ka exact template nahi hota.
    • High valuations future returns ko pressure kar sakti hain, lekin timing certainty se predict nahi hoti.
    • Forecast ko fact na samjhein; assumptions aur uncertainty note karein.
    Study topic 9 of 30

    Portfolio allocation aur balance

    Portfolio policy ka matlab stocks, bonds aur cash ke beech risk manage karna hai.

    • Near-term expenses ko volatile assets par depend na rakhein.
    • Stocks growth potential dete hain; bonds volatility manage karne mein madad kar sakte hain, dono mein risk hai.
    • Ek fixed allocation sabke liye sahi nahi; periodic review karein.
    Study topic 10 of 30

    Defensive investor ke liye common stocks

    Graham defensive investor ke liye diversification, financial strength aur reasonable valuation par zor dete hain.

    • Sirf popular ya fast-growing company choose na karein.
    • Company ka size, debt, earnings record aur dividend history jaise filters saath dekhein.
    • Graham ke original numerical filters historical market ke liye the; unhe blindly apply na karein.
    Study topic 11 of 30

    Active investor: kin cheezon se bachein

    Enterprising investor ke liye negative approach ka matlab weak finances, speculative issues aur overhyped securities se savdhaan rehna hai.

    • Debt-heavy companies aur unstable earnings ko extra scrutiny dein.
    • Hot tips aur complex products ko samjhe bina buy na karein.
    • Low price ka matlab undervalued hona nahi; business deteriorating bhi ho sakta hai.
    Study topic 12 of 30

    Active investor: research se opportunity

    Graham active investor ko disciplined research se special opportunities dhoondhne ka framework dete hain.

    • Company filings, balance sheet, cash flow aur industry conditions padhein.
    • Market neglect ya temporary pessimism se opportunity ban sakti hai, par thesis verify karein.
    • Expected return ke saath downside scenario bhi estimate karein.
    Study topic 13 of 30

    Market fluctuations aur Mr. Market

    Market price daily badalta hai; investor ko price aur estimated business value compare karna chahiye.

    • Volatility ko opportunity bhi samjhein aur risk bhi.
    • Buy/sell decision valuation aur portfolio rules se lein.
    • Leverage volatility ko bade loss mein badal sakta hai.
    Study topic 14 of 30

    Investment funds aur diversification

    Funds multiple securities ka exposure de sakte hain, lekin structure, cost aur strategy samajhna zaroori hai.

    • Expense ratio, tracking difference, holdings aur concentration check karein.
    • Fund ka naam dekhkar diversification assume na karein; holdings overlap ho sakti hain.
    • Past returns future performance ki guarantee nahi.
    Study topic 15 of 30

    Investment advisers ko evaluate karna

    Adviser useful ho sakta hai, lekin fees, incentives, qualifications aur conflicts samajhna chahiye.

    • Poochhein adviser ko kaise compensation milta hai aur kya commission hai.
    • Guaranteed high returns ya pressure-based selling red flags hain.
    • Written plan, risk explanation aur transparent fees maangein.
    Study topic 16 of 30

    Security analysis: business ko samjho

    Security analysis ka kaam financial statements, business model aur earning capacity ko samajhna hai.

    • Revenue growth akela kaafi nahi; margins, cash flow aur debt dekhein.
    • Accounting profit aur actual cash generation alag ho sakte hain.
    • Industry cyclicality aur competitive position ko valuation mein include karein.
    Study topic 17 of 30

    Per-share earnings ko samajhna

    EPS profit ko shares ke hisaab se dikhata hai, lekin ek saal ka EPS poori story nahi batata.

    • Multi-year earnings record aur unusual one-off gains/losses inspect karein.
    • Share buybacks ya new share issuance EPS ko badal sakte hain.
    • Diluted EPS aur operating cash flow compare karein.
    Study topic 18 of 30

    Companies ki comparison

    Comparable companies ka analysis business quality aur valuation differences samajhne mein help karta hai.

    • Similar industry aur business model wali companies compare karein.
    • Accounting policies, debt aur one-time items adjust kiye bina ratios compare na karein.
    • Best company bhi bad price par poor investment ho sakti hai.
    Study topic 19 of 30

    Defensive stock selection checklist

    Defensive stock selection mein safety, stability, financial strength aur price discipline chahiye.

    • Understandable business aur adequate scale starting filters ho sakte hain.
    • Earnings consistency, manageable debt aur dividend record investigate karein.
    • Valuation test ke bina quality company ko bhi overpay na karein.
    Study topic 20 of 30

    Enterprising stock selection checklist

    Active stock selection mein special research, strict criteria aur patience zaroori hain.

    • Undervalued assets, temporary setbacks ya overlooked businesses investigate karein.
    • Management quality, balance-sheet risks aur recovery ke evidence test karein.
    • Thesis fail hone ke signals pehle define karein; confirmation bias se bachein.
    Study topic 21 of 30

    Convertible securities aur warrants

    Convertible bonds aur warrants hybrid ya option-like features rakhte hain; inhe samjhe bina simple bond ya stock na samjhein.

    • Conversion ratio, strike price, maturity, call provisions aur dilution risk padhein.
    • Share price girne par downside protection limited ho sakti hai.
    • Complexity aur issuer credit risk evaluate karein.
    Study topic 22 of 30

    Case studies se seekhna

    Graham ke company examples accounting, management decisions aur market pricing ke outcomes dikhate hain.

    • Case ko us waqt available information ke basis par judge karein.
    • Failure cases se leverage, governance aur overvaluation ke warning signs identify karein.
    • Historical examples current market ke exact predictions nahi.
    Study topic 23 of 30

    Companies ke pairs compare karna

    Similar companies ko side-by-side dekhne se price, profitability, assets aur capital structure ka farq samajh aata hai.

    • Business quality aur financial risk alag-alag assess karein.
    • Cheap-looking multiple ka reason samjhein: temporary issue ya structural decline?
    • Comparison ki date aur market conditions note karein.
    Study topic 24 of 30

    Shareholders, management aur governance

    Shareholder company ka partial owner hota hai; management aur governance long-term value affect karte hain.

    • Capital allocation, related-party transactions aur executive incentives dekhein.
    • Promises se zyada actual execution aur shareholder treatment par focus karein.
    • Voting rights aur disclosures ownership ka hissa hain.
    Study topic 25 of 30

    Dividend policy aur total return

    Dividend cash return deta hai, lekin dividend dena hamesha value creation ka proof nahi.

    • Dividend sustainability ko earnings, free cash flow aur debt ke against check karein.
    • Profitable reinvestment opportunities hon to retained earnings bhi value create kar sakti hain.
    • High yield ke chakkar mein falling business ya payout risk ignore na karein.
    Study topic 26 of 30

    Margin of safety ko practical banana

    Margin of safety valuation uncertainty ke liye buffer hai; iska size business risk aur estimate confidence par depend karta hai.

    • Intrinsic value ko single precise figure ke bajay range mein estimate karein.
    • Weak balance sheet ya uncertain business mein wider buffer ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.
    • Cheap price alone safety nahi; fraud, decline aur liquidity risk assess karein.
    Study topic 27 of 30

    Balance sheet: financial strength

    Balance sheet company ke assets, liabilities aur shareholders' equity ka snapshot hai.

    • Cash, receivables, inventory aur debt ki quality aur maturity dekhein.
    • Short-term obligations cover karne ki ability check karein.
    • Off-balance-sheet commitments aur contingent liabilities bhi relevant ho sakti hain.
    Study topic 28 of 30

    Cash flow aur earnings quality

    Sustainable business ko accounting profit ke saath cash generate karna bhi aana chahiye.

    • Operating cash flow ko net income ke saath compare karein.
    • Capital expenditure ke baad free cash flow samjhein; industry ke hisaab se interpretation badal sakta hai.
    • Receivables ya inventory unusually badhe hon to earnings quality investigate karein.
    Study topic 29 of 30

    Valuation ratios: context zaroori hai

    P/E, P/B aur dividend yield useful tools hain, lekin akela ratio buy/sell signal nahi.

    • P/E ko growth, cyclicality, debt aur earnings quality ke context mein dekhein.
    • Asset-heavy aur asset-light businesses ke P/B ratios directly comparable nahi.
    • Historical averages aur peer comparisons ko current conditions ke saath interpret karein.
    Study topic 30 of 30

    Diversification aur risk control

    Diversification single-company risk reduce kar sakti hai, par market-wide loss eliminate nahi karti.

    • Different companies, industries aur asset classes ka exposure samjhein.
    • Hidden concentration check karein: same sector, economic driver ya overlapping funds.
    • Position size aisa rakhein ki ek thesis galat hone par plan derail na ho.
    Value investing bhi loss-free nahi hoti. Intrinsic value estimates assumptions par depend karte hain; diversification, valuation discipline aur personal risk tolerance zaroori hain.
