Understanding Market Structure (BOS & CHoCH)

Market structure is the foundation of smart money concepts. Before entering any trade, you must identify whether the market is in a bullish or bearish state.

1. Break of Structure (BOS): Occurs when price continues in the direction of the current trend, breaking a previous high or low.

2. Change of Character (CHoCH): Signals a potential trend reversal when price breaks the opposing structural swing point.

Identifying Order Blocks (OB)

An Order Block represents institutional footprints—the last opposing candle before a strong impulsive move that breaks market structure.

Bullish Order Block: The last down-candle before a strong upward impulsive move.

Bearish Order Block: The last up-candle before a strong downward impulsive move.
