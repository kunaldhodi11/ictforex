Introduction to Candlestick Psychology

Candlesticks are the visual representation of buyer and seller battles in the forex market. Each candle tells a unique story about market sentiment over a specific timeframe.

Bullish Candlestick: Opens low and closes high, indicating strong buying pressure.
Bearish Candlestick: Opens high and closes low, indicating strong selling pressure.
