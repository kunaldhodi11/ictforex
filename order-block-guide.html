How to Identify High-Probability Institutional Order Blocks

Published on Sep 21, 2026 • 6 min read

An Order Block (OB) represents the exact candles where central banks and institutional smart money accumulate their large positions before an aggressive market expansion.

Trading Bullish Order Blocks:

Look for the last down-candle before a massive upward displacement that breaks market structure. When price comes back down to test this zone, it provides high-probability bounce entries.
