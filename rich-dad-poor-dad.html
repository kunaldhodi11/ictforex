Author: Robert Kiyosaki

Chapter 1: Lesson 1 - Ameer log paise ke liye kaam nahi karte

Gareeb aur middle-class log paise ke liye kaam karte hain, jabki amir logon ke liye paisa kaam karta hai:

  • Darr aur lalach ki vajah se log Rat Race mein fas jaate hain.
  • Financial education ki kami ke karan log zindagi bhar struggle karte hain.

Chapter 2: Lesson 2 - Assets aur Liabilities ka Farq

Financial freedom ka sabse mukhya niyam:

  • Asset: Jo jeb mein paisa daale (stocks, real estate, business).
  • Liability: Jo jeb se paisa nikaale (expensive car, personal home loan).

Chapter 3: Lesson 3 - Apne kaam se kaam rakho

Apni job ke sath-sath apne asset column ko majboot karna:

  • Apni income ko real assets banane mein invest karein.
  • Apni financial position ko hamesha behtar banate rahein.

Chapter 4: Lesson 4 - Taxes ka Itihaas aur Corporations

Amir log legal tareeqe se apna tax kaise bachate hain:

  • Corporations ke zariye kharche pehle nikalte hain aur bache par tax lagta hai.
  • Financial intelligence tax aur law ki samajh se hi aati hai.

Chapter 5: Lesson 5 - Amir log paise ka aavishkar karte hain

Dimaag hi sabse bada asset hai jo naye mauke pehchanta hai:

  • Risk lene ki kshamta aur confidence hi wealth create karta hai.
  • Darr ko jeet kar naye ideas par kaam karna zaroori hai.

Chapter 6: Lesson 6 - Seekhne ke liye kaam karo, paise ke liye nahi

Naukri sirf salary ke liye nahi, balki naye skills sikhne ke liye karein:

  • Leadership, sales, communication aur financial literacy sabse bade skills hain.
  • Apne gyan ko badhana hi sabse bada nivesh hai.

Chapter 7: Shuruwat kaise karein aur Badhayein

Apne financial safar ko aage badhane ke mukhya kadam:

  • Apne andar ki iccha (passion) ko jagayein aur roz thoda naya seekhein.
  • Sahi mentors aur salahkaron ka chunav karein.
