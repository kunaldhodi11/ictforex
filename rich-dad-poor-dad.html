Introduction: Do Pita (Two Dads) ki Kahani

Robert Kiyosaki ke paas do pita the:

  • Poor Dad: Jo unke asli pita the, padhe-likhe aur ek sarkari karmchari the. Unka vishwas tha ki "Achi padhai karo, ek surakshit naukri dhoondo aur zindagi bhar mehnat karo." Lekin wo hamesha paise ki tangee (financial struggle) mein rehte the.
  • Rich Dad: Jo unke dost ke pita the, unhone zyada padhai nahi ki thi lekin wo Hawaii ke sabse amir vyaktiyon mein se ek bane. Unhone Robert ko paisa kamane aur use manage karne ke real-world niyam sikhaye.

Lesson 1: Ameer log paise ke liye kaam nahi karte

Gareeb aur middle-class log paise ke liye kaam karte hain, jabki amir logon ke liye **paisa unke liye kaam karta hai**.

  • Darr aur Lalach (Fear and Greed): Log naukri sirf isliye karte hain kyunki unhe bill chukane ka darr hota hai, aur jab thode paise milte hain toh lalach mein aakar naye kharche badha lete hain (Rat Race).
  • Financial Education ki Kami: School hamein naukri karna sikhata hai, lekin paisa kaise banayein aur use kaise bachayein, yeh school mein nahi sikhaya jata.

Lesson 2: Assets (Sampatti) aur Liabilities (Karz/Deindari) mein Farq

Financial freedom hasil karne ka sabse pehla niyam hai ki aapko Assets aur Liabilities ka farq pata hona chahiye:

  • Asset (Sampatti): Wo cheez jo aapki jeb mein **paisa daalti hai** (jaise rental real estate, stocks, bonds, business jo aapke bina chale).
  • Liability (Deindari): Wo cheez jo aapki jeb se **paisa nikal leti hai** (jaise aapka personal ghar, nayi gaadi, expensive gadgets, credit card debt).
  • Mukhya Niyam: Gareeb log sirf liabilities kharidt hain, jabki amir log apna dhyaan sirf **Assets** banane par lagate hain.

Lesson 3: Apne kaam se kaam rakho (Mind Your Own Business)

Aapki profession (naukri) aur aapka business alag-alag cheezein hain:

  • Aapko apni naukri ke sath-sath apne financial asset column ko bhi majboot karna chahiye.
  • Apni job mat chhodiye, lekin apni income ko aisi jagah invest kariye jo real assets (jaise stocks ya real estate) kharidne mein madad kare.

Lesson 4: Taxes ka Itihaas aur Corporations ki Taqat

Amir logon ko pata hota hai ki tax ke niyam kaise kaam karte hain aur wo legal tareeqe se apna tax kaise bachate hain:

  • Corporations (Companies) ke zariye business chalane par kharche pehle nikalte hain aur bache hue paise par tax lagta hai, jabki employee pehle tax deta hai aur bache hue paise se kharche chalata hai.

Lesson 5: Amir log paise ka aavishkar karte hain (Invent Money)

Zindagi mein sabse bada asset hamara dimaag hota hai agar ise sahi se train kiya jaye.

  • Har insaan ke andar talent hota hai, lekin darr aur confidence ki kami ki wajah se wo peeche reh jata hai.
  • Jo log risk lene se nahi darte aur naye mauke pehchante hain, wahi asli wealth create karte hain.

Lesson 6: Seekhne ke liye kaam karo, paise ke liye nahi

Naukri sirf isliye mat karo ki wahan se kitni salary mil rahi hai, balki yeh dekho ki wahan se aapko kya naya sikhne ko mil raha hai.

  • Ek behtareen employee banne ke liye alag skills chahiye hoti hain, lekin ek successful business aur investor banne ke liye **leadership, sales, communication aur financial literacy** zaroori hai.
