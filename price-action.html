📈 Module 3: Pure Price Action

Mastering Price Action Trading

Bina kisi complicated indicator ke, sirf chart ki raw movement (Market Structure, Higher Highs, Higher Lows) ko samajhkar professional trading karna sikhein.

Price Action Kya Hai aur Yeh Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Definition: Price Action ek aisi trading method hai jisme trader bina kisi lag-lag indicator (jaise RSI, MACD) ke, sirf live chart ki original candles aur price swings ko dekhakar faisla leta hai ki market kahan jayega.

Market Me Role: Chart par price kabhi bhi seedha upar ya neeche nahi jata; yeh waves (swing highs aur swing lows) banate hue chalta hai. Inhi waves ko padhna hi Price Action kehlata hai.

Uptrend Market Structure: Jab market lagataar naye uche peaks (Higher Highs) aur pichle lows se upar ke dips (Higher Lows) banata hai.

1. Market Structure ko Kaise Samjhein?

Uptrend Structure: Jab market upar jaata hai, to yeh apne pichle high ko tod deta hai (Break of Structure - BOS). Iska matlab buyers strong hain.

Downtrend Structure: Jab market girta hai, to yeh pichle low ko todkar neeche nikal jaata hai, jisse lower lows bante hain.

Trading Rule: Hamesha main trend ki disha me hi trade leni chahiye (Trend is your friend).

2. Swing High aur Swing Low kya hain?

Swing High (Peak): Woh point jahan par price upar jakar achanak neeche mudta hai (Resistance ki tarah kaam karta hai).

Swing Low (Valley): Woh point jahan price girkar achanak upar ki taraf bounce leta hai (Support ki tarah kaam karta hai).

Importance: Inhi swing points par stop-loss rakha jata hai aur entry plan ki jaati hai.
