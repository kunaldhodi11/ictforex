Mastering Price Action Trading

Learn to analyse price action through raw chart movement, including market structure, higher highs, and higher lows, without relying on complicated indicators.

What Is Price Action and How Does It Work?

Definition: Price action is a trading approach in which traders study a chart's candles and price swings to assess market behaviour, rather than relying primarily on indicators such as RSI or MACD.

Market Me Role: Price rarely moves in a straight line. It typically forms waves of swing highs and swing lows. Studying these price movements is the foundation of price action analysis.

Uptrend Market Structure: An uptrend forms when price creates successive higher highs and higher lows.

1. How to Read Market Structure

Uptrend Structure: Price moves upward and breaks its previous high, forming a Break of Structure (BOS). This may indicate buyer strength, subject to confirmation and broader context.

Downtrend Structure: Price moves downward and breaks its previous low, forming lower lows.

Trading Rule: Trade in the direction of the primary trend when your strategy confirms the setup (the trend is your friend).

2. What Are Swing Highs and Swing Lows?

Swing High (Peak): A point where price rises and then turns downward, often acting as resistance.

Swing Low (Valley): A point where price falls and then turns upward, often acting as support.

Importance: These swing points can help define stop-loss placement and trade-entry plans.
