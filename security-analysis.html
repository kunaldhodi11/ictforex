Chapter 1: Security Analysis ka Mool Siddhant (Introduction & Scope)

Yeh kitab value investing ki asli buniyaad hai. Isme sikhaya gaya hai ki stocks ko sirf ek 'paper' ya 'ticker symbol' ki tarah nahi, balki ek real business ki tarah kaise dekha jaye:

  • Investment vs Gambling: Graham aur Dodd kehte hain ki nivesh (investment) wahi hai jahan poori tarah anuman aur analysis ke baad mool dhan (principal) surakshit rahe. Bina padhe paisa lagana jua (gambling) hai.
  • Intrinsic Value (Asli Keemat): Har company ki ek vastavik value hoti hai jo uski assets, earnings aur future growth par nirbhar karti hai. Stock market ke daam is intrinsic value se upar-niche hote rehte hain.

Chapter 2: Financial Statements ka Gahra Adhyayan

Kisi bhi company ke shares kharidne se pehle uske financial records ko kaise padha jaye:

  • Balance Sheet Check: Company par karz (debt) kitna hai aur uske paas assets kitni hain, iski jaanch karna sabse pehla kadam hai.
  • Earnings Power: Company pichle kayi saalon se lagatar munafa (profit) kama rahi hai ya nahi, yeh dekhna zaroori hai.

Chapter 3: Common Stock Analysis (Shares ke Nivesh ke Niyam)

Common stocks (equity) mein nivesh karte samay kin baaton ka dhyan rakhna chahiye:

  • Dividend Record: Aisi companies jo regular dividend deti hain, unki financial health aamtaur par behtar hoti hai.
  • Growth and Stability: Sirf short-term tezi dekhne ke bajaye company ki long-term stability aur management ki imaandari par dhyan dein.

Chapter 4: Margin of Safety (Suraksha ka Ghera)

Security Analysis ka sabse mahatvapurna siddhant jo Warren Buffett ka bhi sabse pasandida hai:

  • Hamesha stock ko uski intrinsic value se kaafi saste daam par kharidein taki agar future mein market mein koi mandi ya galti ho jaye, toh aapka nuksan na ho.
