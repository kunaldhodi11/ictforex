Chapter 1: Security Analysis ka Mool Aadhar

Investment aur gambling ke beech ka bhed aur intrinsic value ki paribhasha:

  • Stocks ko ek paper asset ke bajaye ek business ki tarah samjhein.
  • Bina analysis ke kiye gaya nivesh jua ke saman hai.

Chapter 2: Financial Statements ka Adhyayan

Company ki balance sheet aur income statement ki jaanch karna:

  • Company par karz (debt) ki sthiti ka aaklan karna.
  • Assets aur liabilities ka sateek mulyankan.

Chapter 3: Fixed-Value Investments (Bonds aur Preferred Stocks)

Fixed income securities mein suraksha aur yield ka mahatva:

  • Safety of principal ko sabse upar rakhna.
  • Company ki earning capacity ki aanch karna.

Chapter 4: Senior Securities Analysis with Investment Features

Convertible bonds aur preferred shares ki parakh:

  • Risk aur reward ka sahi santulan banana.
  • Market ki sthiti ke anusaar safety margin dekhna.

Chapter 5: Common Stock Investment ke Mukhya Niyam

Equity shares mein long-term nivesh ke mool niyam:

  • Dividend history aur earnings stability ki jaanch karna.
  • Management ki parardharsita aur imaandari par dhyan dena.

Chapter 6: Company Analysis aur Management ka Mahatva

Industry mein company ki position aur management ki kshamta:

  • Competitive advantage aur market share ka aaklan.
  • Future growth ki sambhavnaon ka vishleshan.

Chapter 7: Margin of Safety siddhant ka Prayog

Security Analysis ka sabse bada nishkarsh:

  • Hamesha intrinsic value se kam daam par kharidari karein.
  • Nuksan ki sambhavna ko shoonya ke kareeb rakhna hi safal niveshak ki pehchan hai.
