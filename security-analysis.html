Security Analysis: The Classic 1934 Edition

Benjamin Graham & David L. Dodd

Benjamin Graham aur David Dodd ki 1934 classic ke major ideas par based 30-topic Hinglish study guide: security analysis, bonds, preferred stocks, common-stock valuation, earnings aur asset values.

    Key concept

    Security analysis ka purpose

    Security analysis ka objective kisi security ke underlying business/claim ko samajhna aur price ke muqable value ka reasoned estimate banana hai.

    • Share ko business ownership claim ke roop mein dekhein, sirf ticker symbol nahi.
    • Bond aur preferred security ke contractual rights common equity se alag hote hain.
    • Analysis uncertainty ko clarify karta hai, future price guarantee nahi karta.
    Key concept

    Financial statements ko jodkar padhein

    Balance sheet, income statement aur cash-flow statement ek doosre ke saath milkar company ki financial position dikhate hain.

    • Revenue growth ke saath margins, working capital aur cash conversion bhi dekhein.
    • Debt maturity, interest coverage aur off-balance-sheet commitments samjhein.
    • Accounting profit aur actual operating cash flow mein farq ho sakta hai.
    Key concept

    Earnings power aur business quality

    Ek saal ke unusual profit ko sustainable earning power samajhna galat ho sakta hai.

    • Multi-year earnings history aur business cycle ka context dekhein.
    • One-off gains, asset sales aur exceptional expenses ko identify karein.
    • Industry structure, competition aur customer concentration assess karein.
    Key concept

    Bonds aur preferred securities

    Fixed-income claims ko evaluate karte waqt principal repayment capacity aur contractual priority central hoti hai.

    • Issuer ki ability to pay interest/principal assess karein.
    • Covenants, seniority, collateral, maturity aur refinancing risk samjhein.
    • Higher yield aksar higher credit risk ka signal ho sakti hai.
    Key concept

    Common stock valuation

    Equity valuation future earning capacity, assets, liabilities aur business risks ke estimate par depend karti hai.

    • Dividend history useful hai, lekin dividend safety aur free cash flow bhi dekhein.
    • Book value ko automatically market value na samjhein; intangible assets aur liabilities matter karte hain.
    • Valuation multiple ko growth, profitability, leverage aur cyclicality ke context mein compare karein.
    Key concept

    Margin of safety aur conclusion

    Conservative assumptions aur purchase price mein buffer rakhna analysis ka important discipline hai.

    • Optimistic forecast ke bajaye base, bear aur bull scenarios banayein.
    • Agar key information unreliable ho to invest na karna bhi valid decision hai.
    • Diversification aur position sizing ke bina achhi analysis bhi portfolio ko risk se nahi bachati.
    Study topic 7 of 30

    Analysis ka scope aur limitations

    Security analysis ka kaam available facts ko evaluate karke security ki value ka reasoned estimate banana hai; yeh certainty ya exact market timing nahi deta.

    • Quantitative facts jaise earnings, assets aur debt ko qualitative facts jaise management aur industry ke saath padhein.
    • Information incomplete, outdated ya misleading ho sakti hai.
    • Analysis ka conclusion assumptions aur evidence ke saath likhein.
    Study topic 8 of 30

    Intrinsic value ka concept

    Intrinsic value business ki earning capacity, assets, financial position aur security ke rights se nikala gaya estimate hai.

    • Market price aur estimated value alag ho sakte hain.
    • Value ko ek exact number ke bajay reasonable range samjhein.
    • Estimate tabhi useful hai jab assumptions conservative aur transparent hon.
    Study topic 9 of 30

    Quantitative aur qualitative factors

    Graham-Dodd approach numbers aur business reality dono ko analyse karta hai.

    • Quantitative: earnings, assets, liabilities, cash flow aur coverage ratios.
    • Qualitative: management, competition, industry structure aur business stability.
    • Strong story weak balance sheet ko automatically fix nahi karti.
    Study topic 10 of 30

    Sources of information

    Analyst ko company accounts ke alawa independent information sources se facts verify karne chahiye.

    • Annual reports, audited statements, bond indentures aur official filings padhein.
    • Industry data, competitors aur historical records se cross-check karein.
    • Management claims ko independent evidence ke bina fact na maan lein.
    Study topic 11 of 30

    Investment aur speculation ka farq

    Book investment ko careful analysis, principal ki safety aur adequate return ke standard se alag karti hai.

    • Speculative outcome ko safe investment ke roop mein present na karein.
    • Security ke legal rights aur downside capacity samjhein.
    • Even rigorous analysis ke baad loss possible hai.
    Study topic 12 of 30

    Securities ki classification

    Different securities ke legal claims aur risk-return profile alag hote hain.

    • Bonds, preferred stocks aur common stocks ko ek jaisa na samjhein.
    • Payment priority, voting rights, collateral aur maturity terms padhein.
    • Security ka naam nahi, uske contract aur issuer ki capacity matter karti hai.
    Study topic 13 of 30

    Fixed-value investments: safety first

    Fixed-value securities ka central test promised interest aur principal ko time par pay karne ki issuer ki ability hai.

    • Issuer ki earning power aur cash resources assess karein.
    • Debt burden, maturity schedule aur refinancing risk check karein.
    • High coupon apne aap safety ka proof nahi.
    Study topic 14 of 30

    Bond investment ke standards

    Bond analysis mein income coverage, asset protection aur issue terms ko saath dekhna chahiye.

    • Interest coverage ko normalised earnings ke saath calculate karein.
    • Secured aur unsecured debt ke claims ka farq samjhein.
    • Covenants aur seniority default ke waqt important hote hain.
    Study topic 15 of 30

    Railroad aur public-utility bonds

    1934 edition mein railroads aur utilities ke liye industry-specific bond analysis discuss hota hai; unke cash flows aur assets alag nature ke hote hain.

    • Regulation, capital expenditure aur operating stability ko samjhein.
    • Industry-specific risks ko generic ratios se replace na karein.
    • Historical sector examples ko aaj ke market par seedha apply na karein.
    Study topic 16 of 30

    Special factors in bond analysis

    Bondholder ke liye issuer ki financial structure aur competing claims ka analysis zaroori hai.

    • Total debt aur fixed charges ko earning power ke against dekhein.
    • Subsidiary guarantees aur inter-company obligations inspect karein.
    • Weak earnings ke dauran interest payment kitni resilient hai, test karein.
    Study topic 17 of 30

    Preferred stock ka structure

    Preferred stock equity hota hai lekin dividend aur liquidation priority common stock se pehle ho sakti hai; terms issue-wise vary karte hain.

    • Cumulative ya non-cumulative dividend terms samjhein.
    • Voting rights, redemption aur conversion clauses padhein.
    • Preferred dividend bond interest jaisi guaranteed payment nahi hoti.
    Study topic 18 of 30

    Preferred stocks ka selection

    Preferred security ko select karte waqt dividend coverage aur issuer ki overall financial strength important hai.

    • Dividend obligations aur senior debt ko combined context mein assess karein.
    • Asset backing aur protective provisions inspect karein.
    • Market yield ko credit aur liquidity risk ke saath compare karein.
    Study topic 19 of 30

    Income bonds aur guaranteed securities

    Income bonds aur guaranteed securities mein payment conditions aur guarantee ki legal strength carefully samajhni hoti hai.

    • Income bond interest payment earnings ya specified conditions par contingent ho sakta hai.
    • Guarantee dene wali entity ki credit strength verify karein.
    • Guarantee wording, scope aur enforceability padhein.
    Study topic 20 of 30

    Protective covenants aur remedies

    Covenants bondholders ko kuch issuer actions se protection de sakte hain, lekin enforcement terms par depend karta hai.

    • Debt limits, asset sales, dividend restrictions aur reporting obligations dekhein.
    • Default remedies aur trustee ke role ko samjhein.
    • Covenant hona risk-free investment ka proof nahi.
    Study topic 21 of 30

    Junior capital aur preferred-stock protection

    Senior claims ke neeche aane wali securities ko protect karne ke liye capital cushion aur contractual rights matter karte hain.

    • Junior capital kitna loss absorb kar sakta hai, analyse karein.
    • Preferred stock protections aur dividend arrears provisions padhein.
    • Capital structure mein har claim ki priority samjhein.
    Study topic 22 of 30

    Investment holdings ki supervision

    Security buy karne ke baad bhi issuer ki condition aur original investment thesis monitor karna zaroori hai.

    • Earnings, debt, covenant compliance aur credit events track karein.
    • Sirf price decline par react na karein; underlying facts dobara evaluate karein.
    • Material deterioration par portfolio risk aur position size review karein.
    Study topic 23 of 30

    Speculative features wale senior issues

    Kuch senior securities mein conversion, participation ya other speculative features ho sakte hain; unka risk plain bond se alag hota hai.

    • Base contractual claim aur speculative upside ko alag analyse karein.
    • Conversion terms, dilution aur issuer solvency check karein.
    • Upside feature ke chakkar mein principal risk ignore na karein.
    Study topic 24 of 30

    Common stock: dividend factor

    1934 edition common-stock theory mein dividend record ko important factor ke roop mein examine karti hai.

    • Dividend history stability ka evidence ho sakti hai, future payment ki guarantee nahi.
    • Payout ko earnings, cash flow aur debt ke against evaluate karein.
    • Dividend yield ko total investment return ke barabar na samjhein.
    Study topic 25 of 30

    Common stock: earnings factor

    Common stock valuation mein sustainable earning power central hai; temporary peak earnings ko permanent na maan lein.

    • Multi-year results aur business cycle ka context dekhein.
    • Exceptional items aur accounting changes normalise karein.
    • Earnings ki quality aur repeatability assess karein.
    Study topic 26 of 30

    Income account ka analysis

    Income statement ke items ko detail mein padhkar actual operating performance aur fixed obligations samjhe ja sakte hain.

    • Revenue, operating expenses, interest, taxes aur net income ka relation dekhein.
    • Depreciation aur unusual charges ko context ke saath analyse karein.
    • Consolidated subsidiaries aur minority interests ko ignore na karein.
    Study topic 27 of 30

    Earnings quality aur normalisation

    Reported earnings mein temporary gains/losses ya cyclical peaks ho sakte hain; valuation ke liye normalised earnings estimate karein.

    • Several years ka record use karein, sirf best year nahi.
    • Asset-sale gains aur one-time credits ko recurring earnings se alag karein.
    • Recession aur boom dono scenarios mein earning power test karein.
    Study topic 28 of 30

    Earnings multiplier aur valuation

    Earnings multiplier (P/E) market price ko per-share earnings ke relation mein dikhata hai, lekin suitable multiple context par depend karta hai.

    • Earnings stable hain ya cyclical, pehle yeh samjhein.
    • Growth, debt, business quality aur interest-rate environment matter karte hain.
    • Low P/E automatic bargain nahi; earnings collapse ka signal bhi ho sakta hai.
    Study topic 29 of 30

    Balance-sheet analysis

    Balance sheet assets, liabilities aur equity ka snapshot deti hai; asset quality aur obligations ko detail mein dekhna chahiye.

    • Cash, receivables, inventory aur fixed assets ki realisability assess karein.
    • Current liabilities, long-term debt aur contingent claims identify karein.
    • Book value ko market value ya liquidation value samajhne ki galti na karein.
    Study topic 30 of 30

    Asset values aur liquidation perspective

    Asset value analysis mein reported book values ke peeche actual economic value aur possible realisation costs samajhne hote hain.

    • Inventory obsolete ho sakta hai; receivables collect na hon; fixed assets ko discount par bechna pad sakta hai.
    • Intangible assets aur hidden liabilities estimate ko materially badal sakte hain.
    • Liquidation value ek scenario hai, guaranteed recovery nahi.
    1934 edition historical work hai. Aaj ke accounting standards, markets, regulation aur financial instruments alag ho sakte hain. Current company filings aur modern standards ke saath concepts verify karein.
