Security Analysis: The Classic 1934 Edition

Benjamin Graham & David L. Dodd

Fundamental analysis, financial statements, earnings power, debt claims aur valuation discipline par high-level study notes. Neeche ke headings learning themes hain, original book ke chapter titles nahi.

Important: Yeh ICT Forex Academy ke original educational study notes hain — poori kitab nahi, aur na hi publisher ke official chapter-by-chapter reproduction. Book ke exact chapter titles/sequence ka daava nahi kiya gaya hai. Detailed understanding ke liye original edition padhein.
Key concept

Security analysis ka purpose

Security analysis ka objective kisi security ke underlying business/claim ko samajhna aur price ke muqable value ka reasoned estimate banana hai.

  • Share ko business ownership claim ke roop mein dekhein, sirf ticker symbol nahi.
  • Bond aur preferred security ke contractual rights common equity se alag hote hain.
  • Analysis uncertainty ko clarify karta hai, future price guarantee nahi karta.
Key concept

Financial statements ko jodkar padhein

Balance sheet, income statement aur cash-flow statement ek doosre ke saath milkar company ki financial position dikhate hain.

  • Revenue growth ke saath margins, working capital aur cash conversion bhi dekhein.
  • Debt maturity, interest coverage aur off-balance-sheet commitments samjhein.
  • Accounting profit aur actual operating cash flow mein farq ho sakta hai.
Key concept

Earnings power aur business quality

Ek saal ke unusual profit ko sustainable earning power samajhna galat ho sakta hai.

  • Multi-year earnings history aur business cycle ka context dekhein.
  • One-off gains, asset sales aur exceptional expenses ko identify karein.
  • Industry structure, competition aur customer concentration assess karein.
Key concept

Bonds aur preferred securities

Fixed-income claims ko evaluate karte waqt principal repayment capacity aur contractual priority central hoti hai.

  • Issuer ki ability to pay interest/principal assess karein.
  • Covenants, seniority, collateral, maturity aur refinancing risk samjhein.
  • Higher yield aksar higher credit risk ka signal ho sakti hai.
Key concept

Common stock valuation

Equity valuation future earning capacity, assets, liabilities aur business risks ke estimate par depend karti hai.

  • Dividend history useful hai, lekin dividend safety aur free cash flow bhi dekhein.
  • Book value ko automatically market value na samjhein; intangible assets aur liabilities matter karte hain.
  • Valuation multiple ko growth, profitability, leverage aur cyclicality ke context mein compare karein.
Key concept

Margin of safety aur conclusion

Conservative assumptions aur purchase price mein buffer rakhna analysis ka important discipline hai.

  • Optimistic forecast ke bajaye base, bear aur bull scenarios banayein.
  • Agar key information unreliable ho to invest na karna bhi valid decision hai.
  • Diversification aur position sizing ke bina achhi analysis bhi portfolio ko risk se nahi bachati.
1934 edition historical work hai. Aaj ke accounting standards, markets, regulation aur financial instruments alag ho sakte hain. Current company filings aur modern standards ke saath concepts verify karein.
