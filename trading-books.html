Mahatvapurna Soochna: Yeh sabhi books ki koi poori kitab (Full Book) nahi hain. In sabhi books mein se mukhya aur important notes nikal kar list banayi gayi hai, taki aap trading ke concepts ko aasani se samajh saken.

Niche di gayi list mein se aap jis bhi book ke notes padhna chahte hain, uske naam par click karein:

Author: Benjamin Graham

1. The Intelligent Investor: The Definitive Book on Value Investing →

Mr. Market ka concept, Margin of Safety, value investing aur Defensive vs Enterprising investor ki strategies.

Author: Benjamin Graham & David L. Dodd

2. Security Analysis: The Classic 1934 Edition →

Warren Buffett ki ultimate favorite "Bible" of investing, Intrinsic value, financial statements analysis aur stock valuation ke mukhya niyam.

Author: Robert Kiyosaki

3. Rich Dad Poor Dad: What the Rich Teach Their Kids About Money →

Assets vs Liabilities ka farq, financial education ka mahatva aur paise ko apne liye kaam karane ke mukhya niyam.

Author: Colin Bennett

4. Trading Volatility: Correlation, Term Structure and Skew →

Options, volatility, smile, skew aur term structure ke mukhya notes aur trading strategies.

Author: Tom Hougaard (Trader Tom)

5. The First Trading Manual: Foundation Course and Strategies →

24-hour trading cycle, Price action principles, Candlestick patterns, 4-bar fractal, aur trading psychology ke mahatvapurna notes.
