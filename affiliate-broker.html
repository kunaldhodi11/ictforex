Important regulatory notice for people in India

As of the RBI Alert List published on 22 October 2024, the Reserve Bank of India listed OctaFX and XM among entities/websites that were neither authorised as persons to deal in forex under FEMA nor authorised to operate forex electronic trading platforms (ETPs) in India. The RBI states that its Alert List is not exhaustive and that absence from the list does not establish authorisation. This dated reference is not a live regulatory-status check. Do not use these links to open or fund a forex/CFD account for transactions from India unless you have independently confirmed that the specific legal entity, platform and proposed transaction are permitted under current Indian law. Check the latest RBI information and obtain qualified legal advice if unsure.
