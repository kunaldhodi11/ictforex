The First Trading Manual: Foundation Course and Strategies

Tom Hougaard

Beginner-friendly 30-topic Hinglish trading guide: market structure, price action, chart timeframes, trade planning, order execution, risk management, trading psychology aur performance review.

    Key concept

    Market sessions aur daily preparation

    Different market sessions mein liquidity, volatility aur participation change ho sakti hai.

    • Forex market week ke dauran global sessions ke overlap mein active rehta hai; exact timing daylight-saving changes ke saath badal sakti hai.
    • London/New York overlap mein activity badh sakti hai, lekin har din same volatility nahi hoti.
    • Economic calendar, major news, spread aur trading plan check karke hi session start karein.
    Key concept

    Trend aur market structure

    Price swings se trend aur range ko identify karne ki basic approach.

    • Uptrend mein higher highs aur higher lows ka sequence dikhta hai; downtrend mein lower highs aur lower lows.
    • Range mein price defined high-low area ke beech oscillate karta hai; breakout confirm hone se pehle false break possible hai.
    • Trend label ko timeframe ke saath specify karein — H4 trend aur M5 structure alag ho sakte hain.
    Key concept

    Price action aur context

    Candles ko isolated signals ki tarah nahi, surrounding structure aur location ke context mein padhein.

    • Strong close momentum dikha sakta hai, lekin next candle continuation guarantee nahi karti.
    • Support/resistance, recent swing points, volatility aur news context ko combine karein.
    • Falling market mein sirf 'cheap' lagne ki wajah se buy na karein; reversal evidence ka wait karein.
    Key concept

    Candlestick formations

    Candlestick patterns buyer-seller interaction ka visual summary dete hain; unki reliability location aur confirmation par depend karti hai.

    • Doji/spinning top indecision dikha sakte hain; akela pattern reversal confirm nahi karta.
    • Hammer/shooting star jaise candles meaningful tab hote hain jab trend aur key level ka context ho.
    • Large body candle momentum dikha sakti hai; entry se pehle stop distance aur reward-to-risk assess karein.
    Key concept

    Entry trigger, stop loss aur trade management

    Ek repeatable trading plan mein setup, trigger, invalidation aur exit pehle se defined hone chahiye.

    • Entry se pehle likhein: setup kya hai, kis condition par trade invalid hoga, SL kahan hoga aur target ka logic kya hai.
    • Stop loss market structure/volatility ke mutabik place karein; arbitrary fixed pip distance har pair par fit nahi hoti.
    • Risk per trade ko account size aur stop distance se calculate karein; loss recover karne ke liye size na badhayein.
    Key concept

    Psychology, routine aur review

    Consistency process se aati hai, ek winning trade ya high win-rate claim se nahi.

    • Trading journal mein setup, entry, exit, screenshot, risk aur emotional state record karein.
    • Loss ke baad revenge trading aur win ke baad overconfidence se bachein.
    • Strategy ko sufficient sample size par evaluate karein; win rate ke saath average win/loss, drawdown aur costs bhi dekhein.
    Study topic 7 of 30

    Trading plan and written rules

    Trading plan setup selection, timing, risk, management aur review ko written rules mein convert karta hai.

    • Entry aur exit conditions objective rakhein.
    • No-trade conditions bhi define karein.
    • Rules ko live capital se pehle historical aur demo data par test karein.
    Study topic 8 of 30

    Choosing a market and instrument

    Har instrument ka spread, volatility, liquidity aur trading hours alag hote hain.

    • Forex pairs, indices aur commodities ke behaviour ko compare karein.
    • Ek waqt mein limited instruments par focus karein.
    • Contract size, tick value aur trading costs samjhein.
    Study topic 9 of 30

    Timeframes and multi-timeframe context

    Higher timeframe broader context deta hai; lower timeframe execution detail de sakta hai.

    • Analysis aur execution timeframe pehle select karein.
    • Timeframes ke signals conflict kar sakte hain.
    • Lower timeframe noise ko higher timeframe context se filter karein.
    Study topic 10 of 30

    Support and resistance zones

    Support/resistance areas woh prices hain jahan buying ya selling interest pehle visible raha ho.

    • Exact single line ke bajaye zone consider karein.
    • Repeated tests level ko strong bhi bana sakte hain aur break bhi kar sakte hain.
    • Break ke baad retest possible hai, guaranteed nahi.
    Study topic 11 of 30

    Breakouts and false breakouts

    Breakout defined range ya level ke bahar price move hota hai; false breakout wapas range mein aa sakta hai.

    • Candle close aur follow-through observe karein.
    • Volume, volatility aur news context dekhein.
    • Breakout chase karne ke bajaye invalidation define karein.
    Study topic 12 of 30

    Pullbacks and continuation

    Pullback trend ke against temporary retracement ho sakta hai; continuation setup confirmation maangta hai.

    • Pullback depth aur structure context assess karein.
    • Trend intact hone ka evidence dekhein.
    • Har retracement trend continuation nahi hota.
    Study topic 13 of 30

    Momentum and market conditions

    Momentum price movement ki speed/strength ko describe karta hai.

    • Strong momentum mein counter-trend trades risky ho sakte hain.
    • Momentum indicators lagging ya noisy ho sakte hain.
    • Trend, range aur high-volatility conditions ko alag treat karein.
    Study topic 14 of 30

    Volatility and stop distance

    Volatility batati hai price kitna move kar sakta hai; stop distance ko market conditions ke saath adapt karna hota hai.

    • ATR jaise tools recent range ka context de sakte hain.
    • Bahut tight stop normal market noise se hit ho sakta hai.
    • Stop widen karte waqt position size reduce karein.
    Study topic 15 of 30

    Position sizing and account risk

    Position size account risk, stop distance aur instrument value ke basis par calculate hota hai.

    • Risk amount trade se pehle decide karein.
    • Pip/tick value aur contract specifications verify karein.
    • Leverage available hona safe risk ka proof nahi.
    Study topic 16 of 30

    Reward-to-risk and expectancy

    Reward-to-risk planned reward ko planned risk se compare karta hai; expectancy outcomes ka average edge estimate karti hai.

    • High RR akela profitable system prove nahi karta.
    • Win rate, average win/loss aur trading costs saath evaluate karein.
    • Realistic sample size aur drawdown include karein.
    Study topic 17 of 30

    Order types and execution

    Market, limit aur stop orders different execution trade-offs offer karte hain.

    • Market order speed deta hai, price guarantee nahi.
    • Limit order price control deta hai, fill guarantee nahi.
    • Stop order trigger hone par slippage possible hai.
    Study topic 18 of 30

    Spread, commission and slippage

    Trading costs strategy ke net result ko materially affect karte hain.

    • Spread session aur news ke waqt widen ho sakta hai.
    • Commission aur swap/financing charges include karein.
    • Backtest mein realistic slippage assumptions use karein.
    Study topic 19 of 30

    Economic calendar and news risk

    Economic releases volatility, spreads aur execution conditions ko quickly change kar sakte hain.

    • High-impact events pehle identify karein.
    • Unexpected headlines ke liye gap risk samjhein.
    • News trade na karna bhi valid plan decision hai.
    Study topic 20 of 30

    Trading sessions and liquidity

    Session overlap activity badha sakta hai, lekin liquidity aur volatility pair ke hisaab se differ karti hai.

    • London/New York hours mein kuch pairs active ho sakte hain.
    • Asian session ko automatically low-volatility na samjhein.
    • Broker time aur daylight-saving shifts verify karein.
    Study topic 21 of 30

    Trading journal and statistics

    Journal decisions aur outcomes ko objective evidence mein convert karta hai.

    • Setup, screenshot, risk, exit aur emotions note karein.
    • R-multiple, expectancy aur drawdown track karein.
    • Small sample se strategy conclusions na nikalein.
    Study topic 22 of 30

    Backtesting and forward testing

    Backtesting historical data par rules check karta hai; forward testing unseen/live demo data par process evaluate karta hai.

    • Rules ko test ke beech repeatedly change na karein.
    • Spread, commission aur slippage include karein.
    • Overfitting aur look-ahead bias se bachein.
    Study topic 23 of 30

    Trading discipline and patience

    Discipline ka matlab valid setup ka wait karna aur rules follow karna hai.

    • FOMO entries aur impulsive trades avoid karein.
    • No-trade day bhi successful discipline ho sakta hai.
    • Routine ko manageable aur repeatable rakhein.
    Study topic 24 of 30

    Emotional control after losses

    Losses trading ka normal part hain; emotional reaction risk ko multiply kar sakta hai.

    • Predefined daily loss limit consider karein.
    • Loss recover karne ke liye lot size na badhayein.
    • Break lekar journal review karein.
    Study topic 25 of 30

    Managing winning trades

    Winning trade ko manage karne ke liye exit logic entry jitna hi important hai.

    • Target, trailing stop ya structure-based exit pehle define karein.
    • Stop ko bina reason door na karein.
    • Partial exits ke costs aur effect ko test karein.
    Study topic 26 of 30

    Common beginner mistakes

    Common errors mein overtrading, excessive leverage, random indicators aur plan changes shamil hain.

    • Ek simple setup par focus karein.
    • Social media signals ko independently verify karein.
    • Guaranteed profit ya fixed win-rate claims par bharosa na karein.
    Study topic 27 of 30

    Building a repeatable routine

    Pre-market, during-market aur post-market routine execution ko consistent banata hai.

    • Session se pehle calendar aur levels review karein.
    • Trade ke waqt checklist use karein.
    • Session ke baad journal aur screenshots update karein.
    Study topic 28 of 30

    Capital preservation and drawdown

    Capital preservation long-term participation ke liye essential hai.

    • Consecutive losses ke scenarios calculate karein.
    • Drawdown mein risk reduce karne ke rules define karein.
    • Emergency savings ko trading capital se alag rakhein.
    Study topic 29 of 30

    Demo trading and gradual transition

    Demo account platform aur strategy practice ke liye useful hai, lekin live emotions aur fills differ karte hain.

    • Demo mein consistent rule-following demonstrate karein.
    • Live transition par smallest practical risk use karein.
    • Performance aur comfort ke basis par gradual progress karein.
    Study topic 30 of 30

    Reviewing and improving a strategy

    Strategy improvement evidence-based honi chahiye, random indicator additions se nahi.

    • Weakness ko journal statistics se identify karein.
    • Ek time par ek change test karein.
    • Market regime change par assumptions revalidate karein.
    Trading mein koi pattern ya setup guaranteed nahi hota. Book ke concepts ko current market par test karein, demo/paper trading se practice karein aur har trade ka risk pehle define karein.
