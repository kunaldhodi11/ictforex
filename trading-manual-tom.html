The First Trading Manual: Foundation Course and Strategies

Tom Hougaard

Market structure, trading routine, price action, candlestick reading, execution aur trading psychology par beginner-friendly learning notes.

Important: Yeh ICT Forex Academy ke original educational study notes hain — poori kitab nahi, aur na hi publisher ke official chapter-by-chapter reproduction. Book ke exact chapter titles/sequence ka daava nahi kiya gaya hai. Detailed understanding ke liye original edition padhein.
Key concept

Market sessions aur daily preparation

Different market sessions mein liquidity, volatility aur participation change ho sakti hai.

  • Forex market week ke dauran global sessions ke overlap mein active rehta hai; exact timing daylight-saving changes ke saath badal sakti hai.
  • London/New York overlap mein activity badh sakti hai, lekin har din same volatility nahi hoti.
  • Economic calendar, major news, spread aur trading plan check karke hi session start karein.
Key concept

Trend aur market structure

Price swings se trend aur range ko identify karne ki basic approach.

  • Uptrend mein higher highs aur higher lows ka sequence dikhta hai; downtrend mein lower highs aur lower lows.
  • Range mein price defined high-low area ke beech oscillate karta hai; breakout confirm hone se pehle false break possible hai.
  • Trend label ko timeframe ke saath specify karein — H4 trend aur M5 structure alag ho sakte hain.
Key concept

Price action aur context

Candles ko isolated signals ki tarah nahi, surrounding structure aur location ke context mein padhein.

  • Strong close momentum dikha sakta hai, lekin next candle continuation guarantee nahi karti.
  • Support/resistance, recent swing points, volatility aur news context ko combine karein.
  • Falling market mein sirf 'cheap' lagne ki wajah se buy na karein; reversal evidence ka wait karein.
Key concept

Candlestick formations

Candlestick patterns buyer-seller interaction ka visual summary dete hain; unki reliability location aur confirmation par depend karti hai.

  • Doji/spinning top indecision dikha sakte hain; akela pattern reversal confirm nahi karta.
  • Hammer/shooting star jaise candles meaningful tab hote hain jab trend aur key level ka context ho.
  • Large body candle momentum dikha sakti hai; entry se pehle stop distance aur reward-to-risk assess karein.
Key concept

Entry trigger, stop loss aur trade management

Ek repeatable trading plan mein setup, trigger, invalidation aur exit pehle se defined hone chahiye.

  • Entry se pehle likhein: setup kya hai, kis condition par trade invalid hoga, SL kahan hoga aur target ka logic kya hai.
  • Stop loss market structure/volatility ke mutabik place karein; arbitrary fixed pip distance har pair par fit nahi hoti.
  • Risk per trade ko account size aur stop distance se calculate karein; loss recover karne ke liye size na badhayein.
Key concept

Psychology, routine aur review

Consistency process se aati hai, ek winning trade ya high win-rate claim se nahi.

  • Trading journal mein setup, entry, exit, screenshot, risk aur emotional state record karein.
  • Loss ke baad revenge trading aur win ke baad overconfidence se bachein.
  • Strategy ko sufficient sample size par evaluate karein; win rate ke saath average win/loss, drawdown aur costs bhi dekhein.
Trading mein koi pattern ya setup guaranteed nahi hota. Book ke concepts ko current market par test karein, demo/paper trading se practice karein aur har trade ka risk pehle define karein.
