Chapter 1: The 24-Hour Trading Cycle (24-Gante ka Trading Chakra)

Market linear nahi hota hai, balki yeh 2 steps forward aur 1 step back ki tarah chalta hai[cite: 17]. Isme global markets aur alag-alag sessions ka mukhya yogdan hota hai:

  • Sunday Night & Asian Session: Sunday raat ko New Zealand aur Australia se currency market khulti hai[cite: 18], aur baad mein Asian markets (Nikkei, Shanghai) ka trend global markets par asar daalta hai[cite: 22].
  • European Session (06:00 - 08:00 GMT): European centers (Frankfurt, London) khulne par volatility tezi se badhti hai, aur German DAX index poore din ke stock market ka rukh tay karta hai[cite: 18].
  • US Session (14:30 GMT): US market khulne par bade moves aur economic news (jaise Non-Farm Payrolls aur GDP) aati hain, jo market mein tezi laati hain[cite: 23].

Chapter 2: Basic Technical Analysis & Trend Definition

Technical analysis ka mukhya aadhar Charles Dow (Dow Theory) par tayaar kiya gaya hai[cite: 26]. Isme market ki direction ko pehchanna sabse zaroori hai:

  • Up-Trend (Tezi): Jab market "Higher Highs" aur "Higher Lows" banata hai[cite: 28, 29].
  • Down-Trend (Mandi): Jab market "Lower Highs" aur "Lower Lows" banata hai[cite: 29]. Selling short ka matlab hai pehle bechna aur baad mein kam daam par wapas kharidna[cite: 30].
  • Range-Bound Market: Jab market na toh upar jata hai aur na hi niche, balki ek दायरे (range) mein ghumta rehta hai[cite: 36].

Chapter 3: Price Action & Price Behaviour Principles

Tom Hougaard ke anusar price action ke 4 mukhya niyam hain jo har trader ko pata hone chahiye[cite: 38]:

  • Principle 1 ("Value Doesn't Exist"): Financial markets mein koi bhi cheez "cheap" ya "expensive" nahi hoti; yahan sirf **"The Price"** hoti hai[cite: 41]. Girti hui stock ko sasta samajh kar buy karna khatarnak ho sakta hai kyunki wo zero bhi ho sakti hai (jaise Northern Rock ya Lehman Brothers)[cite: 41, 46].
  • Principle 2 ("Follow the Trend"): Hamesha long-term trend ki disha mein trade karein aur short-term counter-moves ko fade karein[cite: 47].
  • Principle 3 ("The End of Trend"): Trends tabhi khatam hote hain jab market mein emotional spikes, volume spikes ya panic/euphoria ki sthiti aati hai[cite: 53, 54].
  • Principle 4 ("Ranging to Trending"): Market hamesha ranging se trending aur fir wapas ranging phase mein badalta rehta hai[cite: 55].

Chapter 4: Trend Indicators & Daily Work Routine

Market ke trend ko pehchanne aur daily preparation ke liye kuch behtareen tools hain:

  • Three Bar Swing (3-Bar Rule): Yeh ek simple mechanical indicator hai jisme pichle 3 bars ke high ya low ke break hone par trend change ka pata chalta hai[cite: 71, 73].
  • Daily Work Routine: Subah uthkar economic calendar (Forex Factory) par red-flagged news check karna aur चुsinda currency pairs (jaise EURUSD, GBPUSD, EURJPY) par focus karna chahiye[cite: 20, 76].

Chapter 5: Candlestick Charting & The Essential 8 Patterns

Candlesticks buyers aur sellers ke beech ki ladai ko darshati hain[cite: 78]. Tom Hougaard ke anusar 8 zaroori patterns yeh hain[cite: 84]:

  • 1. Standard Pattern: Continuation pattern jiska close strong hota hai[cite: 86].
  • 2. Spinning Top: Chhota body aur lambi tails wala neutral pattern jo pause ya consolidation dikhata hai[cite: 87].
  • 3. Doji (Long-Legged, Gravestone, Dragonfly): Yeh powerful reversal/turning points hote hain[cite: 89, 90].
  • 4. Umbrella (Hammer & Hanging Man): Support ya resistance par reversal ka sanket dete hain[cite: 95].
  • 5 & 6. Shaven Top/Bottom: Jinki ek taraf tail nahi hoti[cite: 98].
  • 7. Marabozu: Bina shadow wala poora solid candle jo strong momentum dikhata hai[cite: 99].
  • 8. Inverted Hammer & Shooting Star: Top ya bottom par trend reversal ka powerful signal[cite: 101].

Chapter 6: Trade Entry Techniques (4-Bar Fractal & Divergence)

Market mein sahi entry lene ke liye mechanical methods ka istemal kiya jata hai:

  • 4-Bar Fractal: Isme current bar (1) ka close pichle bar (2) aur 3 bars pehle ke bar (4) ke high/low ko cross karta hai, jo ki ek reliable trend change / entry trigger hai[cite: 120].
  • Divergence Strategy: Jab price naya high/low banaye lekin Stochastics Oscillator uske ulta chale, toh yeh trend continuation ya reversal ka strong warning signal deta hai[cite: 121, 124].

Chapter 7: Managing Your Risk & Trading Psychology

Trading mein technique sirf 10% hai aur psychology 90% hai[cite: 13, 169]:

  • Risk Management: Hamesha stop-loss ka use karein[cite: 131]. Ek trade mein apne account ka sirf 1% ya 2% risk lein[cite: 130].
  • The Need to Be Right vs Making Money: Newcomers ko "sahi hone" ki bhookh hoti hai, jabki professional traders ko "paisa banane" se matlab hota hai[cite: 170].
  • Control Your Mind: Apne dimaag ko discipline mein rakhein, perfection ke piche na bhagein, aur apne trading plan ko sakhti se follow karein[cite: 170, 174].
