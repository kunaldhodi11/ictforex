What is a Fair Value Gap (FVG) and How to Trade It?

Published on Sep 21, 2026 • 5 min read

In Smart Money Concepts (SMC), a Fair Value Gap (FVG) is an imbalance created in price action when institutional banks push price aggressively in one direction.

Key Characteristics of FVG:

  • It consists of 3 consecutive expansion candles.
  • There is an empty price inefficiency space between Candle 1's wick and Candle 3's wick.
  • Price frequently retraces back to fill this gap before continuing its primary trend.
