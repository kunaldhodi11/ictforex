📖 Por qué importan las velas japonesas

Candlestick charts were invented by Japanese rice trader Homma Munehisa in the 1700s. He discovered that market prices are not just driven by supply and demand — but also by emotions of traders. Every candle tells you 4 crucial things:

O
Apertura
Where price started
H
High
Highest reached
L
Low
Lowest reached
C
Cierre
Where price ended

🔑 Key principle: A candlestick pattern is only meaningful when it appears at a key price level (support, resistance, or a major swing point). A Hammer in the middle of nowhere means nothing. A Hammer at a key support = high-probability trade.

🕯️ Anatomía of a Candlestick

Every candlestick has 3 parts: the Cuerpo, Mecha superior (Shadow), and Mecha inferior. The body shows the range between Apertura and Cierre. Wicks show how far price went beyond the body but was rejected.

Green / Alcista
Cierre ABOVE Apertura. Compradores won the session.
Red / Bajista
Cierre BELOW Apertura. Vendedores won the session.
Wick (Shadow)
Precio went there but was rejected. Long wick = strong rejection.

Familia Doji

A Doji forms when Apertura ≈ Cierre. It shows temporary balance or indecision, not an automatic reversal. Its meaning depends on the preceding trend, nearby levels and what price does next.

1. Standard Doji
Apertura = Cierre. Equal wicks above and below. Neither buyers nor sellers won. Perfect balance.
📍 Señal: Wait for next candle to confirm direction.
2. Dragonfly Doji 🐉
Apertura = Cierre = High. Very long lower wick. Compradores completely rejected the low prices and pushed price back to the top.
📍 Entrada: BUY above next bullish candle. SL below the Doji low.
3. Gravestone Doji ⚰️
Apertura = Cierre = Low. Long upper wick. Vendedores completely rejected the high prices and pushed back down to the bottom.
📍 Entrada: SELL below next bearish candle. SL above Doji high.
4. Long-Legged Doji
Very long wicks both ways, tiny/no body. Wild session — both sides tried but failed. Market is confused.
📍 Señal: Major move coming. Watch for breakout.
5. Four Precio Doji
Apertura = High = Low = Cierre. Just a flat horizontal line. Extremely rare. Seen in very low-volume markets or news lockdown periods.
📍 Señal: Complete market halt. Avoid trading.
Señales de posible giro

Familia Hammer

Hammer patterns have a small body and a long wick on one side. They signal strong rejection of a price level. The SAME shape can be bullish or bearish depending on where it appears in the trend.

6. Hammer 🔨
Small body at top. Long lower wick = 2x or 3x the body size. Compradores pushed price back up from a low. Found at the parte baja de una tendencia bajista.
📍 Entrada: BUY above next green candle. SL = below hammer low.
7. Inverted Hammer
Small body at bottom. Long upper wick. Compradores tried to push higher. Appears at parte baja de una tendencia bajista. Weaker than Hammer — needs confirmation.
📍 Entrada: Wait for next green candle to confirm before buying.
8. Hanging Man 🪝
Same shape as Hammer but appears at parte alta de una tendencia alcista. Vendedores tested lower prices. Weakness is showing — trend may reverse down.
📍 Entrada: SELL below next red candle. SL above Hanging Man high.
9. Shooting Star ⭐
Small body at bottom. Long upper wick = 2x+ body. At parte alta de una tendencia alcista. Vendedores strongly rejected higher prices. A possible bearish rejection signal; confirm with location, trend context and follow-through.
📍 Entrada: SELL on next red candle. SL above Shooting Star high.
Momentum

Marubozu

Marubozu means "bald" in Japanese — no wicks at all. This is a strong momentum candle in which one side controlled most or all of the candle range. It does not guarantee continuation.

10. Alcista Marubozu

Sin mechas. Abre en el mínimo y cierra en el máximo. Los compradores dominaron prácticamente toda la sesión. Momentum alcista fuerte, aunque no garantiza una subida posterior.

Cuerpo itself becomes key support zone. Buy pullbacks to this body.
11. Bajista Marubozu

Sin mechas. Abre en el máximo y cierra en el mínimo. Los vendedores dominaron prácticamente toda la sesión. Momentum bajista fuerte, aunque no garantiza una caída posterior.

Cuerpo becomes key resistance. Sell any rally into this body zone.
Indecision & Momentum

Spinning Top & Belt Hold

12. Spinning Top (Alcista)
Small body, equal wicks both sides. Both buyers and sellers active but neither dominates. Market is hesitating.
At support = bullish reversal possible.
13. Spinning Top (Bajista)
Same as bullish version but a red body. At resistance levels this signals a potential reversal downward.
At resistance = bearish reversal possible.
14. Alcista Belt Hold
Aperturas at the low of the session (no lower wick), closes near the high. Strong bullish candle. Compradores controlled from the very open.
📍 Alcista signal. Enter BUY on next candle.
15. Bajista Belt Hold
Aperturas at the high of the session (no upper wick), closes near the low. Vendedores controlled from the very open. Strong bearish signal.
📍 Bajista signal. Enter SELL on next candle.

Patrones Engulfing

One of the most reliable and widely-used reversal patterns. The second candle completely engulfs (covers) the entire body of the first candle — showing a complete shift in who is in control.

16. Alcista Engulfing 🟢

At the parte baja de una tendencia bajista: green candle's body fully covers the previous red candle's body. Compradores took complete control. Potential bullish reversal; wait for confirmation and assess the surrounding market structure.

✅ Entrada: BUY on close of green candle
🛑 SL: Below the low of the pattern
🎯 TP: Next resistance; only take the setup if the planned target offers acceptable risk-to-reward.
17. Bajista Engulfing 🔴

En la parte alta de una tendencia alcista, el cuerpo de la vela roja cubre por completo el cuerpo de la vela verde anterior. Puede indicar que los vendedores están ganando fuerza. Es un posible giro bajista: espera confirmación y analiza la estructura del mercado.

✅ Entrada: SELL on close of red candle
🛑 SL: Above the high of the pattern
🎯 TP: Next support; only take the setup if the planned target offers acceptable risk-to-reward.
Posible giro (requiere confirmación)

Patrones Harami

"Harami" means pregnant in Japanese. The first candle is large, the second is small and fits inside the first candle's body like a baby. It signals momentum is fading but needs confirmation.

18. Alcista Harami
Large red candle, then small green inside it. Bajista momentum slowing. At support = possible bottom. Needs next candle to confirm.
Wait for 3rd green candle. Then BUY.
19. Bajista Harami
Large green candle, then small red inside it. Alcista momentum slowing. At resistance = possible top. Needs next candle to confirm.
Wait for 3rd red candle. Then SELL.
20. Alcista Harami Cross
Same as Alcista Harami but the second candle is a Doji. Stronger signal because the Doji shows complete indecision after the strong sell.
Stronger bullish reversal than regular Harami.
21. Bajista Harami Cross
Same as Bajista Harami but the second candle is a Doji. Stronger bearish signal because Doji shows complete indecision after the strong buy.
Stronger bearish reversal than regular Harami.
Posible giro de precisión

Patrones Tweezer

Dos velas forman máximos casi iguales (Tweezer Top) o mínimos casi iguales (Tweezer Bottom). El precio ha probado una zona similar más de una vez; esto puede indicar rechazo, pero no demuestra actividad institucional.

22. Tweezer Bottom 📌

Both candles reach the same low. Precio tested that level twice and was rejected. Strong buyers at that exact price. BUY above 2nd candle. SL below twin lows.

23. Tweezer Top 📌

Both candles reach the same high. Precio tried to break higher twice but was rejected. Strong sellers defending that level. SELL below 2nd candle. SL above twin highs.

Posible giro moderado

Piercing Line & Dark Cloud Cover

24. Piercing Line

Large red candle. Then green candle opens BELOW the red's low but closes ABOVE 50% of the red body. Compradores fighting back. BUY on confirmation.

25. Dark Cloud Cover

Large green candle. Then red candle opens ABOVE the green's high but closes BELOW 50% of the green body. Vendedores fighting back. SELL on confirmation.

Patrón de dos velas de fuerte impacto

Patrones Kicker

Kicker es un patrón de dos velas basado en un gap, en el que la segunda vela abre con fuerza en dirección contraria. Puede reflejar un cambio repentino del sentimiento del mercado, a menudo relacionado con noticias. Los gaps reales son poco frecuentes en Forex spot y pueden volver a probarse.

26. Alcista Kicker 🚀

Aparece una vela roja y después el precio abre con un gap al alza formando una vela verde. El gap no se vuelve a probar en este ejemplo. Puede reflejar un impulso alcista fuerte, a menudo tras una noticia.

27. Bajista Kicker 📉

Aparece una vela verde y después el precio abre con un gap a la baja formando una vela roja. El gap no se vuelve a probar en este ejemplo. Puede reflejar un impulso bajista fuerte.

Posible giro

Meeting Lines (Counterattack)

Two candles where the second candle closes at the same price as the first. After an opposite-colored large candle, the second candle reverses and closes at the same level.

28. Alcista Meeting Lines

Después de una vela roja grande, la vela verde abre más abajo, pero cierra en el MISMO precio que la vela roja. Puede indicar que los compradores absorbieron la presión vendedora. Es una posible señal alcista.

29. Bajista Meeting Lines

Después de una vela verde grande, la vela roja abre más arriba, pero cierra en el MISMO precio que la vela verde. Puede indicar que los vendedores absorbieron la presión compradora. Es una posible señal bajista.

Patrones Star

Star patterns are 3-candle formations and among the most reliable reversal signals in all of technical analysis. Widely trusted by institutional traders globally.

30. Morning Star ⭐ (Alcista)
1

Vela bajista grande: los vendedores dominan el movimiento

2

Vela pequeña (star): indecisión y posible pérdida de fuerza de los vendedores

3

Vela alcista grande que cierra por encima del punto medio de la primera vela

✅ Entrada: BUY on close of candle 3
🛑 SL: Below candle 2 low
🎯 TP: Next meaningful resistance; check whether the planned risk-to-reward is acceptable.
31. Evening Star 🌙 (Bajista)
1

Vela alcista grande: los compradores dominan el movimiento

2

Vela pequeña en la parte alta: indecisión y posible agotamiento de los compradores

3

Vela bajista grande que cierra por debajo del punto medio de la primera vela

✅ Entrada: SELL on close of candle 3
🛑 SL: Above candle 2 high
🎯 TP: Next meaningful support; check whether the planned risk-to-reward is acceptable.
32. Morning Doji Star ⭐ (Stronger)

Es similar al Morning Star, pero la vela central es un Doji que refleja indecisión. Puede reforzar la hipótesis de giro porque muestra una pausa antes de que los compradores intenten tomar el control.

Stronger than regular Morning Star. Enter BUY on candle 3 close.
33. Evening Doji Star 🌙 (Stronger)

Es similar al Evening Star, pero la vela central es un Doji. Puede reforzar la hipótesis de giro bajista al mostrar una pausa antes de que los vendedores intenten tomar el control.

Stronger than regular Evening Star. Enter SELL on candle 3 close.
Posible continuación de tendencia

Three Soldiers & Three Crows

34. Three White Soldiers 🏹

3 consecutive large green candles, each closing higher than the previous and each opening within the prior body. Strong uptrend continuation. Don't chase — buy the pullback.

35. Three Black Crows 🦅

3 consecutive large red candles, each closing lower. Strong downtrend signal. Appears after a top or during a breakdown. Sell any rally. Bajista momentum is very strong.

Confirmed Posible giro

Three Inside & Three Outside

These are Harami and Engulfing patterns confirmed by a 3rd candle. Much more reliable because the reversal is proven — not just potential.

36. Three Inside Up
Red → Small Green inside → Big Green breaking above. Confirmed bullish reversal. Enter BUY on 3rd candle close.
37. Three Inside Down
Green → Small Red inside → Big Red breaking below. Confirmed bearish reversal. Enter SELL on 3rd candle close.
38. Three Outside Up

Un Alcista Engulfing de dos velas seguido de una tercera vela que cierra aún más arriba aporta confirmación adicional de un posible giro alcista.

Engulfing + confirmation = a possible BUY setup, not a guarantee.
39. Three Outside Down

Bajista Engulfing (2 candles) then a 3rd candle that closes even lower = confirmed downtrend reversal. Most reliable of the three-candle bearish patterns.

Engulfing + confirmation = a possible SELL setup, not a guarantee.
Rare & Extremely Powerful

Abandoned Baby

Abandoned Baby es una formación poco frecuente de tres velas en la que un Doji queda separado de las velas cercanas por gaps a ambos lados. Los gaps son poco comunes en Forex spot, por lo que los ejemplos perfectos aparecen con menor frecuencia. Espera confirmación: ningún patrón está garantizado.

40. Alcista Abandoned Baby 👶

Vela roja grande → gap bajista → Doji aislado → gap alcista → vela verde grande. Los dos gaps separan el Doji de las velas vecinas. Es una posible señal alcista que requiere confirmación.

✅ Entrada: BUY on close of green candle
🛑 SL: Below the Doji low
🎯 Min R:R: 1:4
41. Bajista Abandoned Baby 👶

Vela verde grande → gap alcista → Doji aislado → gap bajista → vela roja grande. Los dos gaps separan el Doji de las velas vecinas. Es una posible señal bajista que requiere confirmación.

✅ Entrada: SELL on close of red candle
🛑 SL: Above the Doji high
🎯 Min R:R: 1:4
Rare & Avanzado Patróns
Avanzado

Rare & Avanzado Patróns

Estos patrones aparecen con menos frecuencia, pero pueden encontrarse en mercados reales. Aprende a reconocer sus estructuras y estudia siempre su contexto. Conocer un patrón no garantiza una ventaja ni un resultado favorable.

42. Three Line Strike (Alcista)

3 consecutive red candles followed by 1 large green candle that engulfs all 3. Counter-intuitive: this is actually a bullish continuation. Bears exhausted, bulls take over.

Potential BUY setup after the fourth bullish candle closes; wait for confirmation and a suitable entry location.
43. Three Line Strike (Bajista)

Tres velas alcistas consecutivas son seguidas por una vela bajista grande que revierte el avance anterior. Es la contraparte bajista de la formación alcista; el patrón por sí solo no demuestra actividad institucional.

Potential SELL setup after the 4th bearish candle closes; wait for confirmation and a suitable entry location.
44. Upside Gap Two Crows

Aparece una vela verde grande y después dos velas rojas pequeñas que abren con gap al alza, pero permanecen por encima de la vela verde. Puede advertir de un posible giro bajista cerca de la parte alta.

Bajista reversal. Watch for breakdown below the gap.
45. Stick Sandwich

Aparece una vela roja, después una verde y luego otra roja que cierra al MISMO precio que la primera roja. La vela verde queda entre las rojas. Puede sugerir un rechazo de precios más bajos, pero necesita confirmación.

Alcista reversal. Double-bottom forming at same close price.
46. Advance Block

Tres velas verdes similares a White Soldiers, pero cada una tiene un cuerpo progresivamente menor y mechas superiores más largas. Puede mostrar pérdida de momentum comprador y advertir de una posible pausa o giro bajista.

Warning sign at resistance. Reduce longs, watch for reversal.
47. High Wave Candle

Cuerpo pequeño con mechas muy largas en AMBOS lados. Muestra una volatilidad elevada sin dirección clara: el precio subió y bajó mucho, pero cerró cerca de la apertura. Refleja indecisión.

Avoid trading. Wait for clarity. Major move incoming.
⚡ Módulo de entrada

How to Trade Patrones de velas

A pattern alone means nothing. It's the location + pattern + confirmation that creates a high-probability trade. Use this 3-step system for every trade:

1

Ubicación

Is the pattern forming at a key support, resistance, or major swing level? A pattern at a meaningful support/resistance area or swing point is generally more useful than the same shape in the middle of a range.

2

Patrón

Is there a valid candlestick pattern? Must show clear rejection or reversal energy at the key level. The candle must be complete — never enter on an unfinished candle.

3

Confirmación

Wait for the next candle to confirm the direction. Enter on close of confirmation candle. Never FOMO — if you miss it, the market will give you another setup.

Ejemplos de gráficos reales

Real Chart Examples — NAS100, XAUUSD & US30

Estudia cómo se comportan las velas dentro del movimiento real del mercado. Estos ejemplos anotados de TradingView incluyen velas alcistas y bajistas largas, Doji, formaciones Hammer, Pin Bars alcistas y bajistas y Shooting Stars. Analiza tanto la vela marcada como el movimiento del precio que la rodea: el contexto convierte una forma en información útil.

01 · NAS100 · 15-minute

Lectura del rechazo y el momentum durante una sesión

Qué muestra el gráfico: A strong bearish candle drives price lower, while a bullish Pin Bar and a Hammer later show rejection from lower prices. A Doji marks a pause, followed by a long bullish candle and a bearish Pin Bar near the later high.

Cómo interpretarlo: The lower-wick rejections suggest buyers are defending an area, but the Doji alone is indecision—not a buy signal. The long bullish candle shows stronger buying participation; the later bearish Pin Bar warns that buyers may be meeting supply.

Confirmación: Look for a close above the rejection candle high for bullish follow-through, or a break below the bearish Pin Bar low for bearish follow-through. Always check the nearby swing levels.

02 · NAS100 · 15-minute

Doji, Shooting Star y expansión fuerte de velas

Qué muestra el gráfico: A Doji appears during a sideways phase, a Shooting Star is marked after an upward push, and long bullish candles appear during the recovery. Long bearish candles later accompany a sharp sell-off.

Psicología del mercado: The Doji reflects a temporary balance between buyers and sellers. The Shooting Star shows an attempt to trade higher that was rejected. Large candle bodies show decisive momentum, but do not by themselves prove that a trend will continue.

Confirmación: For a Shooting Star, watch for a bearish close or a break of its low. For bullish continuation, look for price to hold above the impulse candle's base and form a higher low.

03 · NAS100 · 5-minute

Hammer y Pin Bar alcista durante una recuperación

Qué muestra el gráfico: A Hammer and a bullish Pin Bar are marked after downward movement, with a Doji appearing near a later intraday low. Long bullish candles then appear as price advances.

Psicología del mercado: The long lower shadows show that sellers pushed price down but could not keep it there. The following bullish expansion suggests buyers gained control after the rejection.

Confirmación: A close above the Hammer or Pin Bar high adds evidence of a reversal. A retest that holds the rejection area can offer a more controlled entry than chasing the first large green candle.

04 · NAS100 · 5-minute

Presión bajista, rechazo Hammer y recuperación

Qué muestra el gráfico: Several long bearish candles appear during the decline. A Hammer is marked near the low, followed by a strong upward reaction. A bearish Pin Bar is also marked earlier near a local high.

Psicología del mercado: The bearish candles show strong selling pressure. Near the low, the Hammer indicates that lower prices were rejected; the rebound shows buyers responding, although the pattern needs confirmation.

Confirmación: Watch whether price closes above the Hammer high and breaks the nearest lower high. If price instead breaks the Hammer low, the bullish-reversal idea is invalidated.

05 · NAS100 · 5-minute

Rechazos Pin Bar dentro de un movimiento bajista

Qué muestra el gráfico: A Doji and bearish Pin Bar appear near the early part of the chart. A bullish Pin Bar is marked during a later recovery, while long bearish candles appear near the right side before price drops sharply.

Cómo interpretarlo: A bullish Pin Bar can create a short-term bounce without reversing the broader structure. The later bearish expansion is a reminder to distinguish a local reaction from a confirmed trend change.

Confirmación: Compare each rejection with the latest swing high and low. A bullish idea needs a higher high or a clear bullish break; a bearish idea gains support if price breaks the rejection candle low.

06 · NAS100 · 15-minute

Velas de momentum y Shooting Star en un máximo

Qué muestra el gráfico: Long bullish candles appear during an upward leg. A Doji signals a pause, a Shooting Star is marked close to the local high, and a long bearish candle appears later as price sells off.

Psicología del mercado: Compradores initially move price higher with strong bodies. The Shooting Star shows rejection of higher prices, while the later bearish expansion confirms that sellers were able to push price lower in this example.

Confirmación: Do not sell simply because a Shooting Star appears. Wait for a bearish close or a break below its low, ideally with resistance or a prior high nearby.

07 · NAS100 · 15-minute

Múltiples rechazos alrededor de swing points intradía

Qué muestra el gráfico: A Doji and bullish Pin Bar appear during the earlier decline. A long bullish candle marks a forceful upward move. A bearish Pin Bar near the later high is followed by a sell-off, and a bullish Pin Bar and Hammer appear near the low.

Psicología del mercado: The chart demonstrates that rejection candles can appear on both sides of the market. Their meaning depends on location: the bearish rejection near a high precedes weakness, while lower-wick rejections near the low precede a recovery.

Confirmación: Use the nearby swing structure. A bullish setup should reclaim a short-term high; a bearish setup should break a recent low. Avoid treating every wick as an automatic reversal.

08 · US30 · 15-minute

Momentum Expansion and a Sharp Bajista Posible giro

Qué muestra el gráfico: Long bullish candles appear as US30 advances through the marked level. A Doji forms near the upper part of the move, followed by long bearish candles and a sharp decline.

Psicología del mercado: The bullish impulse reflects strong buying. The Doji indicates hesitation after the advance, but the subsequent bearish expansion—not the Doji alone—is what demonstrates that sellers took control in this sample.

Confirmación: Watch for a break of the Doji's local support or a lower high after the pause. A Doji can also precede continuation, so wait for price to reveal direction.

09 · US30 · 15-minute

Pin Bar bajista, Doji y rechazo alcista

Qué muestra el gráfico: A bearish Pin Bar and Doji are marked after an early rise. Long bearish candles accompany the decline, while a bullish Pin Bar appears close to the low before a strong recovery and long bullish candles.

Psicología del mercado: The upper-wick rejection suggests buyers could not hold the higher prices. Near the low, the bullish Pin Bar shows lower prices being rejected; the subsequent rally provides follow-through in this example.

Confirmación: For a bullish reversal, look for price to break a nearby lower high after the Pin Bar. For bearish continuation, a fresh low and failed retest would support the sellers' case.

10 · XAUUSD · 15-minute

Oro: impulso alcista seguido de rechazo bajista

Qué muestra el gráfico: A long bullish candle appears during an upward push. A bearish Pin Bar is marked near the high, followed by long bearish candles and a Doji during the decline. Another long bullish candle appears near the later low.

Psicología del mercado: The chart captures a shift from buying momentum to rejection at higher prices. The Doji during the decline is a pause, while the later bullish candle shows a forceful response from lower levels.

Confirmación: At the high, look for a close below the bearish Pin Bar low. Near the low, wait for a bullish close and a structure break before assuming the decline has reversed.
