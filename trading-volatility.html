    Key concept

    Historical volatility vs implied volatility

    Volatility price returns ke variation ka measure hai; direction nahi batati.

    • Historical volatility (HV) past realized returns se calculate hoti hai; result chosen period aur calculation method par depend karta hai.
    • Implied volatility (IV) option prices se infer hoti hai aur market ki forward-looking uncertainty/pricing ko reflect karti hai; yeh guaranteed forecast nahi.
    • High IV ka matlab market zaroor upar ya neeche jayega nahi — sirf options mein expected movement/risk premium zyada price ho sakta hai.
    Key concept

    Options basics: calls, puts and premium

    Call buyer ko underlying buy karne ka right aur put buyer ko sell karne ka right deta hai, contract terms ke mutabik.

    • Call bullish exposure de sakta hai aur put bearish/hedging exposure, lekin profit direction ke saath entry premium, strike, expiry aur IV par bhi depend karta hai.
    • Option buyer ka maximum loss aam taur par paid premium hota hai; option writer ka risk structure ke hisaab se bahut bada ho sakta hai.
    • Moneyness, time to expiry, liquidity, bid-ask spread aur volatility sab price ko affect karte hain.
    Key concept

    Option Greeks aur pricing

    Option models theoretical prices estimate karte hain; real market prices supply, demand aur model assumptions se bhi influence hote hain.

    • Delta: underlying price ke chhote move ke liye option price sensitivity ka estimate.
    • Gamma: delta kitni tezi se badalta hai; expiry ke paas ATM options mein gamma risk badh sakta hai.
    • Vega: implied volatility change ke prati sensitivity; Theta: time decay; Rho: interest-rate sensitivity.
    • Greeks estimates hain, fixed guarantees nahi; market conditions aur model assumptions matter karte hain.
    Key concept

    Volatility smile aur skew

    Different strike prices ke options ki implied volatility ek jaisi nahi hoti; is pattern ko smile ya skew kehte hain.

    • Equity index markets mein downside puts par IV kabhi-kabhi higher hoti hai, jo crash protection demand aur tail-risk pricing se linked ho sakti hai.
    • Pattern asset class, expiry aur market regime ke saath badalta hai; har market mein same shape assume na karein.
    • Skew ko sirf bullish/bearish signal na samjhein.
    Key concept

    Volatility term structure: contango and backwardation

    Term structure same underlying ke different expiries ki implied volatility compare karti hai.

    • Upward-sloping term structure ko aam taur par contango kaha jata hai: longer-dated IV shorter-dated IV se higher hoti hai.
    • Backwardation mein near-term IV longer-dated IV se higher hoti hai; event risk ya market stress ek reason ho sakta hai.
    • Yeh labels curve ki shape batate hain, future realized volatility ki guarantee nahi.
    Key concept

    Correlation and diversification

    Correlation batata hai do return series kitni saath move karti hain; coefficient aam taur par -1 se +1 ke beech hota hai.

    • +1 perfect positive co-movement, 0 linear relationship nahi, aur -1 perfect negative co-movement ko indicate karta hai.
    • Diversification index/portfolio volatility ko reduce kar sakti hai jab holdings perfectly correlated na hon; correlation crisis mein badal sakti hai.
    • Index volatility individual constituent stocks se aksar kam ho sakti hai, lekin yeh correlation ki definition nahi — weights, covariances aur diversification ka combined effect hai.
    Key concept

    Volatility strategies and risk

    Strategies volatility exposure, direction, time decay aur event risk ko alag-alag tareeke se combine karti hain.

    • Long straddle/strangle mein large move ki zaroorat hoti hai; move ki timing aur IV crush bhi result ko affect karte hain.
    • Iron condor/credit spreads range-bound market mein premium collect karne ki koshish karte hain, lekin sharp move aur gap risk se loss ho sakta hai.
    • Position size, max loss, liquidity, expiry risk aur stress scenario pehle calculate karein.
    Study topic 8 of 30

    Realized volatility measurement

    Realized volatility historical returns ke dispersion ko measure karti hai; estimate sampling aur annualisation se badalta hai.

    • Daily aur intraday returns alag estimates de sakte hain.
    • Window aur annualisation convention clearly note karein.
    • Outliers aur market closures results distort kar sakte hain.
    Study topic 9 of 30

    Volatility clustering and regimes

    High-volatility periods kabhi high volatility ke saath persist karte hain; volatility constant nahi hoti.

    • Regime changes possible hain.
    • Past regime future guarantee nahi.
    • Risk limits ko conditions ke saath review karein.
    Study topic 10 of 30

    Forecasting volatility

    Volatility forecasts historical data, implied prices aur statistical models se ban sakte hain.

    • Forecast horizon aur inputs match karein.
    • Model error aur sudden events ke liye allowance rakhein.
    • Forecast probability estimate hai, certainty nahi.
    Study topic 11 of 30

    Volatility risk premium

    Implied aur subsequently realized volatility ka difference volatility risk premium ke context mein study hota hai.

    • IV realized volatility se upar ho sakti hai, hamesha nahi.
    • Premium risk bear karne ka compensation ho sakta hai.
    • Short volatility free income nahi hai.
    Study topic 12 of 30

    Volatility surface

    Volatility surface strikes aur expiries ke across implied volatility ka combined view deti hai.

    • Smile/skew aur term structure dono dimensions hain.
    • Liquidity aur interpolation method result affect karte hain.
    • Surface time ke saath shift hoti hai.
    Study topic 13 of 30

    Forward volatility

    Forward volatility future period ki implied volatility ka market-based estimate hai.

    • Forward variance total variance se derive ki ja sakti hai.
    • Expiry dates aur day-count conventions matter karte hain.
    • Yeh future realized volatility ki guarantee nahi.
    Study topic 14 of 30

    Variance swaps

    Variance swap realized variance aur contract strike ke difference par cash settlement dene wala derivative hai.

    • Payoff variance se linked hota hai.
    • Notional, observation period aur settlement formula check karein.
    • Variance aur volatility swap payoffs alag hote hain.
    Study topic 15 of 30

    Volatility swaps

    Volatility swap ka payoff realized volatility aur agreed strike ke difference se linked hota hai.

    • Yeh variance swap se alag instrument hai.
    • Convexity adjustment pricing ko affect kar sakta hai.
    • Contract definitions carefully padhein.
    Study topic 16 of 30

    VIX and volatility indices

    VIX jaise indices option prices se near-term implied volatility ka standardized measure estimate karte hain.

    • VIX S&P 500 options se derived 30-day measure hai.
    • VIX index directly investable asset nahi.
    • VIX futures/ETPs ke returns index se differ kar sakte hain.
    Study topic 17 of 30

    Volatility futures and roll yield

    Volatility futures curve spot index se alag ho sakti hai; futures exposure mein roll effects important hain.

    • Contango mein roll cost ho sakta hai.
    • Backwardation mein roll dynamics reverse ho sakti hain.
    • Spot index aur futures product return same nahi.
    Study topic 18 of 30

    Event risk and implied volatility

    Earnings aur central-bank decisions jaise events option prices mein uncertainty reflect karte hain.

    • Event se pehle IV rise ho sakti hai.
    • Actual move implied move se chhota ya bada ho sakta hai.
    • Expiry aur event timing verify karein.
    Study topic 19 of 30

    Volatility crush

    Event ke baad implied volatility sharply decline ho sakti hai, ise volatility crush kehte hain.

    • Direction sahi hone par bhi option buyer loss kar sakta hai.
    • Event uncertainty premium mein pehle priced ho sakti hai.
    • Entry price, IV aur breakeven saath analyse karein.
    Study topic 20 of 30

    Skew dynamics and tail risk

    Skew different strikes par protection ki relative demand aur tail-risk pricing reflect kar sakta hai.

    • Put skew downside insurance demand se linked ho sakta hai.
    • Skew standalone direction signal nahi.
    • Crash protection expensive aur timing-sensitive ho sakti hai.
    Study topic 21 of 30

    Risk reversals and butterflies

    Risk reversal aur butterfly option combinations hain jo skew aur smile exposure banate hain.

    • Risk reversal call-put relative pricing compare karta hai.
    • Butterfly selected strikes ki curvature exposure deta hai.
    • Expiry aur delta convention clear rakhein.
    Study topic 22 of 30

    Calendar spreads and term-structure trades

    Calendar spreads different expiries ke options buy/sell karke relative time-volatility exposure banate hain.

    • Near-term aur long-term IV difference matter karta hai.
    • Time decay aur event dates important hain.
    • Max risk, margin aur exercise terms check karein.
    Study topic 23 of 30

    Dispersion trading

    Dispersion strategies index volatility aur constituent volatility ke relative pricing differences target karti hain.

    • Index aur single-stock option exposures alag hain.
    • Correlation aur constituent weights result affect karte hain.
    • Hedging aur transaction costs significant ho sakte hain.
    Study topic 24 of 30

    Correlation trading

    Correlation exposure multiple assets ke joint movement aur covariance structure se related hai.

    • Correlation static nahi hoti.
    • Stress periods mein correlation rise kar sakti hai.
    • Basket weights aur composition measure badalte hain.
    Study topic 25 of 30

    Covariance and portfolio volatility

    Portfolio volatility asset volatilities ke saath covariance aur portfolio weights par depend karti hai.

    • Portfolio variance mein covariance terms included hain.
    • Diversification risk reduce kar sakti hai, eliminate nahi.
    • Concentration aur liquidity stress separately assess karein.
    Study topic 26 of 30

    Hedging volatility exposure

    Options aur related instruments se volatility exposure hedge ho sakta hai, lekin hedge perfect nahi hota.

    • Delta hedge price sensitivity reduce karta hai, volatility risk nahi.
    • Vega aur gamma separately monitor karein.
    • Basis, rebalancing aur transaction costs include karein.
    Study topic 27 of 30

    Higher-order Greeks option value ki nonlinear sensitivities explain karte hain.

    • Vanna delta-volatility interaction measure karti hai.
    • Volga/vomma vega ki volatility sensitivity describe karta hai.
    • Charm time ke saath delta change capture karta hai.
    Study topic 28 of 30

    Model risk and assumptions

    Option models simplifying assumptions use karte hain; real markets mein jumps aur liquidity gaps ho sakte hain.

    • Inputs aur calibration source verify karein.
    • Stress scenarios aur alternative models compare karein.
    • Model output executable quote nahi hota.
    Study topic 29 of 30

    Liquidity, bid-ask and execution

    Theoretical value aur actual executable price ke beech meaningful difference ho sakta hai.

    • Bid-ask spread, open interest aur depth check karein.
    • Large orders slippage create kar sakte hain.
    • Exit liquidity entry se pehle consider karein.
    Study topic 30 of 30

    Portfolio construction and stress testing

    Volatility positions ko portfolio ke total risk budget ke andar size karna chahiye.

    • Spot gaps, IV shifts aur correlation jumps test karein.
    • Margin calls aur liquidity needs estimate karein.
    • Maximum loss aur counterparty exposure monitor karein.
    Options aur volatility strategies complex hain. Short-volatility positions mein bahut bada, kabhi-kabhi theoretically unlimited loss ho sakta hai. Yeh notes trading recommendation nahi hain; paper trading aur risk controls ke bina options trade na karein.
