Chapter 1: Volatility ka Parichay (Introduction)

Volatility ka matlab hota hai kisi stock ya market ki price mein kitni tezi se utaar-chadhaw (fluctuation) ho raha hai.

  • Historical Volatility (HV): Yeh pichle (past) data par adharit hoti hai[cite: 3].
  • Implied Volatility (IV): Yeh bhavishya (future) ki ummeed par adharit hoti hai, jo options market mein sabse zyada dekhi jati hai[cite: 3].

Chapter 2: Options Market ki Buniyadi Baatein

Volatility trading karne ke liye options ki samajh hona sabse zaroori hai.

  • Call Option (CE): Jab lagta hai ki market upar jayega, tab ise kharida jata hai (Bullish)[cite: 3].
  • Put Option (PE): Jab lagta hai ki market niche aayega, tab ise kharida jata hai (Bearish)[cite: 3].
  • Mukhya Shabd: Strike Price, Expiry Date, Premium aur Moneyness (ITM, ATM, OTM)[cite: 3].

Chapter 3: Volatility aur Pricing (Black-Scholes aur Greeks)

Options ki sahi keemat nikalne ke liye Black-Scholes model aur Greeks ka prayog hota hai[cite: 3].

  • Black-Scholes Model: Option ki price nikalne ka mukhya ganitiye formula[cite: 3].
  • Vega: Volatility (IV) mein 1% badlav hone par option ke premium par kya asar padega (Volatility traders ke liye yeh sabse khaas hai)[cite: 3].
  • Theta: Samay bitne ke sath option ki value ka kam hona (Time Decay)[cite: 3].

Chapter 4: Volatility Skew aur Smile

Asli market mein sabhi strike prices par volatility ek jaisi nahi hoti[cite: 3].

  • Volatility Smile/Skew: Niche ke strikes (OTM Puts) par volatility aamtaur par zyada hoti hai kyunki traders ko market crash hone ka dar hamesha rehta hai[cite: 3].

Chapter 5: Volatility ka Term Structure

Alag-alag expiry dates ke sath volatility ka aapsi kaisa sambandh hota hai[cite: 3].

  • Contango: Jab chote samay ki volatility kam aur lambe samay ki volatility zyada hoti hai[cite: 3].
  • Backwardation: Jab market mein achanak dar failne par chote samay ki volatility bahut high ho jati hai[cite: 3].

Chapter 6: Correlation Trading

Index aur uske andar ke alag-alag stocks ki volatility ka aapsi mel[cite: 3].

  • Index vs Stock: Index ki volatility aamtaur par individual stocks se kam hoti hai kyunki wahan diversification hota hai[cite: 3].

Chapter 7: Volatility Trading ki Alag-alag Strategies

Market mein trade karne ke liye alag-alag strategies ka istemal kiya jata hai[cite: 3].

  • Straddle & Strangle: Jab market mein kisi bade move ki ummeed ho (Long Volatility)[cite: 3].
  • Iron Condor / Credit Spreads: Jab market range-bound ho aur volatility girne wali ho (Short Volatility)[cite: 3].
