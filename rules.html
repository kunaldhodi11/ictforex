📖 Essential Guidelines

Basic Trading Rules & Common Psychological Mistakes

Trading shuru karne se pehle har beginner ko in rules aur common galtiyo ke baare me pata hona chahiye taaki wo apna capital safe rakh sakein.

1. Overtrading Se Kaise Bochein?

Aksar beginners loss hone ke baad gusse me aakar aur trades le lete hain (Revenge Trading). Yeh sabse badi psychological mistake hai. Aapko apne din ka ek fix target ya max loss limit set karni chahiye.

2. Risk Management (Stop Loss Kitna Hona Chahiye?)

Bina Stop Loss ke trade kabhi mat karo. Hamesha apne total account balance ka 1% ya 2% hi ek trade me risk lo taaki agar market aapke khilaf jaye toh bada nuksan na ho.

3. Market Sentiment Aur Patience

Trading ek business hai, isko game ya juwa (gambling) ki tarah mat lo. Jab tak aapka setup ya confirm order block na dikhe, tab tak entry mat lo. Patience sabse bada hathyar hai.
