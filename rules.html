Basic Trading Rules & Common Psychological Mistakes

Before trading, every beginner should understand these rules and common mistakes to help manage risk and protect capital.

1. How to Avoid Overtrading

Beginners sometimes take additional trades out of frustration after a loss, a behaviour known as revenge trading. This is a common psychological mistake. Set a clear daily objective and a maximum daily loss limit.

2. Risk Management (How Much Stop-Loss Distance Is Appropriate?)

Never trade without a stop-loss. Some traders use a 1%–2% risk limit per trade, but the appropriate level depends on personal circumstances and risk tolerance. A stop loss does not guarantee the exact exit price.

3. Market Sentiment and Patience

Trading is a business; do not treat it like a game or gambling. Do not enter a trade unless your predefined setup and confirmation conditions are present. Patience is one of the most important trading skills.
