Basic Trading Rules aur Common Psychological Mistakes

Trading shuru karne se pehle har beginner ko ye rules aur common mistakes samajhni chahiye, taaki woh apne capital ko behtar tarike se protect kar sake.

1. Overtrading Se Kaise Bachein?

Kai beginners loss hone ke baad gusse mein aur trades lete hain. Isse Revenge Trading kehte hain, aur ye ek common psychological mistake hai. Har din ke liye ek fixed target aur maximum loss limit pehle se set karein.

2. Risk Management (Stop Loss Kitna Hona Chahiye?)

Stop Loss ke bina trade na karein. Ek trade mein apne total account balance ka sirf 1% ya 2% risk karne par vichar karein. Isse market aapke against jaane par potential loss control mein reh sakta hai. Risk percentage apni strategy aur financial situation ke hisaab se tay karein.

3. Market Sentiment Aur Patience

Trading ek business hai. Ise game ya gambling ki tarah na lein. Jab tak aapka trading setup confirm na ho, tab tak entry na lein. Sirf Order Block dikhna trade lene ki guarantee nahi hota. Patience trading ki ek important skill hai.
