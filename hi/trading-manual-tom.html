The First Trading Manual: Foundation Course and Strategies

Tom Hougaard

Beginners ke liye 30 topics ki Hinglish trading guide: Market Structure, Price Action, chart timeframes, trade planning, order execution, Risk Management, Trading Psychology aur performance review.

    Important Concept

    Market Sessions aur Daily Preparation

    Alag-alag market sessions mein liquidity, volatility aur market participation badal sakti hai.

    • Forex market trading week mein global sessions ke dauran active rehta hai. Exact timings daylight-saving changes ke saath badal sakti hain.
    • London aur New York sessions ke overlap mein market activity badh sakti hai, lekin volatility har din ek jaisi nahi hoti.
    • Session shuru karne se pehle Economic Calendar, major news, spread aur apna trading plan check karein.
    Important Concept

    Trend aur Market Structure

    Price swings ki madad se trend aur range identify karne ka basic tareeka.

    • Uptrend mein Higher Highs aur Higher Lows ka sequence dikhta hai. Downtrend mein Lower Highs aur Lower Lows bante hain.
    • Range market mein price ek defined high-low area ke beech move karta hai. Breakout confirm hone se pehle False Break bhi ho sakta hai.
    • Trend ko hamesha timeframe ke saath samjhein — H4 ka trend aur M5 ka structure alag ho sakta hai.
    Important Concept

    Price Action aur Market Context

    Candles ko alag-alag signals ki tarah na dekhein. Unhe surrounding market structure aur price location ke context mein samjhein.

    • Strong candle close momentum dikha sakta hai, lekin isse next candle ki continuation guarantee nahi hoti.
    • Support aur Resistance zones, recent Swing Points, volatility aur news context ko saath mein evaluate karein.
    • Gir rahe market mein sirf price 'cheap' lagne ki wajah se Buy na karein. Reversal ke clear evidence ka wait karein.
    Important Concept

    Candlestick Formations

    Candlestick patterns buyers aur sellers ke interaction ko visually samjhate hain. Inki reliability pattern ki location aur confirmation par depend karti hai.

    • Doji aur Spinning Top market mein indecision dikha sakte hain. Sirf ek pattern reversal confirm nahi karta.
    • Hammer aur Shooting Star jaise candles tab zyada meaningful hote hain jab trend aur important price level ka context bhi ho.
    • Large body wali candle momentum dikha sakti hai. Entry se pehle Stop Loss distance aur Reward-to-Risk zaroor check karein.
    Important Concept

    Entry Trigger, Stop Loss aur Trade Management

    Ek repeatable Trading Plan mein setup, entry trigger, invalidation condition aur exit pehle se define hone chahiye.

    • Entry se pehle likhein: setup kya hai, kis condition mein trade invalid hoga, Stop Loss kahan hoga aur target ka logic kya hai.
    • Stop Loss ko Market Structure aur volatility ke hisaab se place karein. Ek fixed pip distance har currency pair ke liye suitable nahi hoti.
    • Har trade ka risk account size aur Stop Loss distance ke basis par calculate karein. Loss recover karne ke liye Lot Size na badhayein.
    Important Concept

    Trading Psychology, Routine aur Review

    Consistency ek disciplined process se aati hai, kisi ek winning trade ya high win-rate claim se nahi.

    • Trading Journal mein setup, entry, exit, screenshot, risk aur apni emotional state record karein.
    • Loss ke baad Revenge Trading aur win ke baad Overconfidence se bachein.
    • Strategy ko sufficient sample size par evaluate karein. Win Rate ke saath Average Win/Loss, Drawdown aur trading costs bhi check karein.
    Study topic 7 of 30

    Trading Plan aur Written Rules

    Trading Plan mein setup selection, timing, risk, trade management aur review ke written rules hote hain.

    • Entry aur Exit conditions clear aur objective rakhein.
    • Kin conditions mein trade nahi lena hai, ye bhi pehle define karein.
    • Real capital use karne se pehle rules ko historical data aur Demo Account par test karein.
    Study topic 8 of 30

    Market aur Trading Instrument ka Selection

    Har trading instrument ka Spread, Volatility, Liquidity aur trading hours alag hote hain.

    • Forex pairs, Indices aur Commodities ke market behaviour ko compare karein.
    • Ek samay par limited instruments par hi focus karein.
    • Contract Size, Tick Value aur trading costs ko achhi tarah samjhein.
    Study topic 9 of 30

    Timeframes aur Multi-Timeframe Context

    Higher Timeframe market ka broader context deta hai, jabki Lower Timeframe entry execution ki detail de sakta hai.

    • Analysis aur execution ke timeframes pehle hi select karein.
    • Alag-alag timeframes ke signals ek-doosre se conflict kar sakte hain.
    • Higher Timeframe ke context ki madad se Lower Timeframe ke market noise ko filter karein.
    Study topic 10 of 30

    Support aur Resistance Zones

    Support aur Resistance aise price areas hain jahan pehle buying ya selling interest dikh chuka ho.

    • Ek exact line ke bajaye price zone ko consider karein.
    • Kisi level ke repeated tests use important bana sakte hain, lekin level break bhi ho sakta hai.
    • Breakout ke baad retest ho sakta hai, lekin ye guaranteed nahi hai.
    Study topic 11 of 30

    Breakouts aur False Breakouts

    Breakout tab hota hai jab price defined range ya level ke bahar move karta hai. False Breakout mein price wapas range ke andar aa sakta hai.

    • Candle Close aur uske baad price ka Follow-through observe karein.
    • Volume, Volatility aur News Context ko check karein.
    • Breakout ko chase karne ke bajaye pehle invalidation level define karein.
    Study topic 12 of 30

    Pullbacks aur Trend Continuation

    Pullback trend ke opposite ek temporary retracement ho sakta hai. Trend Continuation setup ke liye confirmation zaroori hai.

    • Pullback ki depth aur Market Structure ka context evaluate karein.
    • Check karein ki existing trend abhi bhi intact hai ya nahi.
    • Har retracement ke baad trend continue ho, ye zaroori nahi hai.
    Study topic 13 of 30

    Momentum aur Market Conditions

    Momentum price movement ki speed aur strength ko describe karta hai.

    • Strong momentum ke against trade lena risky ho sakta hai.
    • Momentum Indicators late signals de sakte hain ya market noise dikha sakte hain.
    • Trending, Ranging aur High-Volatility market conditions ko alag tarah se handle karein.
    Study topic 14 of 30

    Volatility aur Stop Loss Distance

    Volatility se pata chalta hai ki price kitna move kar sakta hai. Stop Loss distance ko market conditions ke hisaab se adjust karna chahiye.

    • ATR jaise tools recent price range ka context de sakte hain.
    • Bahut tight Stop Loss normal market noise ki wajah se hit ho sakta hai.
    • Stop Loss distance badhane par position size ko reduce karein.
    Study topic 15 of 30

    Position Sizing aur Account Risk

    Position Size ko account risk, Stop Loss distance aur instrument value ke basis par calculate kiya jata hai.

    • Trade lene se pehle risk amount decide karein.
    • Pip Value, Tick Value aur Contract Specifications verify karein.
    • High Leverage available hone ka matlab ye nahi ki trade ka risk safe hai.
    Study topic 16 of 30

    Reward-to-Risk aur Expectancy

    Reward-to-Risk planned reward ko planned risk se compare karta hai. Expectancy strategy ke average expected outcome ka estimate deti hai.

    • Sirf high RR hone se koi system profitable prove nahi hota.
    • Win Rate, Average Win/Loss aur trading costs ko saath mein evaluate karein.
    • Analysis mein realistic sample size aur Drawdown ko bhi include karein.
    Study topic 17 of 30

    Order Types aur Execution

    Market, Limit aur Stop Orders ke execution benefits aur limitations alag hote hain.

    • Market Order jaldi execute ho sakta hai, lekin exact price ki guarantee nahi hoti.
    • Limit Order price control deta hai, lekin order fill hone ki guarantee nahi hoti.
    • Stop Order trigger hone par Slippage ho sakti hai.
    Study topic 18 of 30

    Spread, Commission aur Slippage

    Trading costs strategy ke final result ko kaafi affect kar sakte hain.

    • Trading session aur news ke waqt Spread badh sakta hai.
    • Commission aur Swap/Financing Charges ko calculation mein include karein.
    • Backtesting mein realistic Slippage assumptions use karein.
    Study topic 19 of 30

    Economic Calendar aur News Risk

    Economic news releases se Volatility, Spread aur order execution conditions jaldi badal sakti hain.

    • High-impact news events ko pehle identify karein.
    • Unexpected news ki wajah se Gap Risk ho sakta hai; ise samjhein.
    • News ke waqt trade na lena bhi ek valid trading decision hai.
    Study topic 20 of 30

    Trading Sessions aur Liquidity

    Do sessions ke overlap se market activity badh sakti hai, lekin Liquidity aur Volatility currency pair ke hisaab se alag hoti hai.

    • London aur New York sessions ke dauran kuch currency pairs zyada active ho sakte hain.
    • Asian Session ko bina analysis ke low-volatility session na samjhein.
    • Broker ka server time aur Daylight-Saving changes verify karein.
    Study topic 21 of 30

    Trading Journal aur Statistics

    Trading Journal aapke decisions aur results ko measurable evidence mein badalta hai.

    • Setup, Screenshot, Risk, Exit aur apni emotions record karein.
    • R-Multiple, Expectancy aur Drawdown track karein.
    • Bahut kam trades ke sample se strategy ke baare mein final conclusion na nikalein.
    Study topic 22 of 30

    Backtesting aur Forward Testing

    Backtesting mein historical data par rules check kiye jate hain. Forward Testing mein naye ya live Demo data par strategy evaluate hoti hai.

    • Testing ke beech rules ko baar-baar change na karein.
    • Spread, Commission aur Slippage ko test mein include karein.
    • Overfitting aur Look-Ahead Bias se bachein.
    Study topic 23 of 30

    Trading Discipline aur Patience

    Discipline ka matlab valid setup ka wait karna aur apne trading rules follow karna hai.

    • FOMO entries aur impulsive trades se bachein.
    • Jis din valid setup na mile aur aap trade na lein, woh bhi disciplined decision hai.
    • Apni routine ko simple, manageable aur repeatable rakhein.
    Study topic 24 of 30

    Loss ke Baad Emotional Control

    Losses trading ka normal hissa hain. Emotional reaction ki wajah se risk aur badh sakta hai.

    • Apne plan mein Daily Loss Limit pehle se define karein.
    • Loss recover karne ke liye Lot Size na badhayein.
    • Break lein aur apna Trading Journal review karein.
    Study topic 25 of 30

    Winning Trades ko Manage Karna

    Winning Trade ko manage karne ke liye Exit Plan bhi Entry Plan jitna important hai.

    • Target, Trailing Stop ya Structure-Based Exit pehle se define karein.
    • Bina valid reason ke Stop Loss ko aur door na karein.
    • Partial Exit ke costs aur overall result par uske effect ko test karein.
    Study topic 26 of 30

    Beginners ki Common Trading Mistakes

    Common mistakes mein Overtrading, Excessive Leverage, bina plan ke Indicators use karna aur baar-baar strategy badalna shamil hain.

    • Ek simple aur clearly defined setup par focus karein.
    • Social Media par mile trading signals ko khud verify karein.
    • Guaranteed Profit ya fixed Win Rate ke claims par bharosa na karein.
    Study topic 27 of 30

    Repeatable Trading Routine Banana

    Market se pehle, trading ke dauran aur market ke baad ki routine aapke process ko consistent banati hai.

    • Session se pehle Economic Calendar aur important price levels review karein.
    • Trade lene se pehle apni checklist use karein.
    • Session ke baad Trading Journal aur screenshots update karein.
    Study topic 28 of 30

    Capital Protection aur Drawdown

    Long-term trading ke liye Capital Protection bahut zaroori hai.

    • Lagataar losses ke possible scenarios calculate karein.
    • Drawdown ke dauran risk reduce karne ke rules pehle se define karein.
    • Emergency Savings ko Trading Capital se alag rakhein.
    Study topic 29 of 30

    Demo Trading aur Dheere-Dheere Live Trading

    Demo Account platform aur strategy practice karne ke liye useful hai, lekin Live Trading mein emotions aur order fills alag ho sakte hain.

    • Demo Account mein consistently apne trading rules follow karein.
    • Live Trading shuru karte waqt sabse chhota practical risk use karein.
    • Apni performance aur comfort ke hisaab se dheere-dheere progress karein.
    Study topic 30 of 30

    Strategy ka Review aur Improvement

    Strategy mein improvement data aur evidence ke basis par karein, random Indicators add karke nahi.

    • Trading Journal ke statistics se strategy ki weaknesses identify karein.
    • Ek baar mein sirf ek change test karein.
    • Market conditions badalne par apni strategy ki assumptions dobara verify karein.
    Koi bhi trading pattern ya setup guaranteed result nahi deta. Book ke concepts ko current market data par test karein, Demo/Paper Trading se practice karein aur har trade ka risk entry se pehle define karein.
