Trading Volatility: Complete Guide

Yeh guide Colin Bennett ki prasiddh pustak "Trading Volatility" par adharit hai, jisme volatility, options pricing, skew, term structure aur trading strategies ko saral bhasha mein samjhaya gaya hai.

Chapter 1: Introduction to Volatility

Volatility kisi financial asset ki price mein hone wale utaar-chadhaw (fluctuation) ki maatra hai jo market mein risk aur uncertainty ko darshati hai.

  • Historical Volatility (HV): Yeh past ke data par adharit hoti hai.
  • Implied Volatility (IV): Yeh future ki expectations par adharit hoti hai aur options pricing mein sabse mahatvapurna hoti hai.

Chapter 2: The Options Market Basics

Options contracts volatility trading ka mukhya adhar hain.

  • Call Option (CE): Market upar jaane par fayda (Bullish).
  • Put Option (PE): Market niche aane par fayda (Bearish).
  • Key Terms: Strike Price, Expiry Date, Premium, aur Moneyness (ITM, ATM, OTM).
  • Value: Intrinsic Value aur Time Value (Theta Decay).

Chapter 3: Volatility & Pricing (Black-Scholes & Greeks)

Options ki fair value nikalne ke liye Black-Scholes model aur Greeks ka istemal hota hai.

  • Black-Scholes Model: Option pricing ka mukhya mathematical model.
  • Delta: Price movement ke prati sensitivity.
  • Gamma: Delta ki badalne ki gati.
  • Vega: Implied Volatility (IV) mein badlav ka asar (Volatility traders ke liye sabse mahatvapurna).
  • Theta: Time decay (samay ke sath option value ka ghatna).

Chapter 4: The Volatility Skew (Smile)

Real market mein sabhi strike prices par IV ek jaisi nahi hoti.

  • Volatility Smile/Skew: OTM Put options ki IV aamtaur par zyada hoti hai kyunki market crash ka dar hamesha bana rehta hai (Crash Phobia).
  • Equity Skew: Stock markets mein niche ke strikes (Puts) par volatility zyada hoti hai, jise skew kehte hain.

Chapter 5: The Term Structure of Volatility

Volatility ka alag-alag expiry dates ke sath kaisa sambandh hota hai.

  • Contango: Jab short-term volatility low aur long-term volatility high hoti hai (normal market sthiti).
  • Backwardation: Jab market mein panic ya crisis ke dauran short-term volatility achanak bahut high ho jati hai.

Chapter 6: Correlation Trading

Index aur uske andar ke individual stocks ki volatility aur correlation ka aapsi sambandh.

  • Index vs Stock Volatility: Index ki volatility aamtaur par individual stocks se kam hoti hai kyunki diversification ka asar hota hai.
  • Correlation Risk: Market girne par stocks ka correlation badh jata hai, jisse index par bada asar padta hai.

Chapter 7: Volatility Trading Strategies

Practical trading ke liye alag-alag strategies ka prayog kiya jata hai.

  • Straddle & Strangle: Jab market mein bade move ki ummeed ho (Long Volatility - High Vega & Positive Gamma).
  • Iron Condor / Credit Spreads: Jab market range-bound ho aur volatility girne wali ho (Short Volatility - Capturing Theta).
  • Calendar Spreads: Alag-alag expiry ke beech volatility aur time decay ka fayda uthana.
