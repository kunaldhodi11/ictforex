Trading Volatility: Mahatvapurna Notes aur Summary

Mahatvapurna Soochana: Yeh koi poori kitab nahi hai. Is page par Colin Bennett ki prasiddh kitab ke mukhya aur zaroori notes diye gaye hain, taaki aap trading ke concepts ko bilkul aasan bhasha mein samajh saken.

Chapter 1: Volatility ka Parichay (Introduction)

Volatility ka matlab hota hai kisi stock ya market ki price mein kitni tezi se utaar-chadhaw (fluctuation) ho raha hai.

  • Historical Volatility (HV): Yeh pichle (past) data par adharit hoti hai ki price pehle kitni hili thi.
  • Implied Volatility (IV): Yeh bhavishya (future) ki ummeed par adharit hoti hai, jo options market mein sabse zyada dekhi jati hai.

Chapter 2: Options Market ki Buniyadi Baatein

Volatility trading karne ke liye options ki samajh hona sabse zaroori hai.

  • Call Option (CE): Jab lagta hai ki market upar jayega, tab ise kharida jata hai (Bullish).
  • Put Option (PE): Jab lagta hai ki market niche aayega, tab ise kharida jata hai (Bearish).
  • Mukhya Shabd: Strike Price, Expiry Date, Premium aur Moneyness (ITM, ATM, OTM).
  • Value: Isme Intrinsic Value aur Time Value (Theta Decay) hoti hai.

Chapter 3: Volatility aur Pricing (Black-Scholes aur Greeks)

Options ki sahi keemat (fair value) nikalne ke liye Black-Scholes model aur Greeks ka prayog hota hai.

  • Black-Scholes Model: Option ki price nikalne ka mukhya ganitiye (mathematical) formula.
  • Delta: Stock ki price badalne par option par kitna asar padega.
  • Vega: Volatility (IV) mein 1% badlav hone par option ke premium par kya asar padega (Volatility traders ke liye yeh sabse khaas hai).
  • Theta: Samay bitne ke sath option ki value ka kam hona (Time Decay).

Chapter 4: Volatility Skew aur Smile

Asli market mein sabhi strike prices par volatility ek jaisi nahi hoti.

  • Volatility Smile/Skew: Niche ke strikes (OTM Puts) par volatility aamtaur par zyada hoti hai kyunki traders ko market crash hone ka dar hamesha rehta hai.

Chapter 5: Volatility ka Term Structure

Alag-alag expiry dates ke sath volatility ka aapsi kaisa sambandh hota hai.

  • Contango: Jab chote samay ki volatility kam aur lambe samay ki volatility zyada hoti hai (normal market sthiti).
  • Backwardation: Jab market mein achanak dar ya panic failne par chote samay ki volatility bahut high ho jati hai.

Chapter 6: Correlation Trading

Index aur uske andar ke alag-alag stocks ki volatility ka aapsi mel.

  • Index vs Stock: Index ki volatility aamtaur par individual stocks se kam hoti hai kyunki wahan diversification hota hai.
  • Correlation Risk: Market girne par sabhi stocks ek sath girte hain, jisse correlation badh jata hai.

Chapter 7: Volatility Trading ki Alag-alag Strategies

Market mein trade karne ke liye alag-alag strategies ka istemal kiya jata hai.

  • Straddle & Strangle: Jab market mein kisi bade move ki ummeed ho (Long Volatility - High Vega).
  • Iron Condor / Credit Spreads: Jab market ek hi range mein rahe aur volatility girne wali ho (Short Volatility - Capturing Theta).
  • Calendar Spreads: Alag-alag expiry ki volatility ka fayda uthana.
