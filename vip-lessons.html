LESSON 01 · ICT CONCEPTS

ICT Market Structure

Meaning: Meaningful swing highs/lows aur directional structure ko identify karein.

Chart par pehchanein: HH/HL ya LH/LL sequence; relevant swing break par close/confirmation dekhein.

Educational plan: Example: bullish bias ke baad swing high break; invalidation recent HL ke neeche.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 02 · ICT CONCEPTS

Market Structure Shift (MSS)

Meaning: MSS possible short-term directional change ka clue hai, guarantee nahi.

Chart par pehchanein: Liquidity event ke baad opposite-side swing ka decisive break aur displacement dekhein.

Educational plan: Example: SSL sweep → bullish MSS; entry confirmation ke baad, SL sweep low ke neeche.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 03 · ICT CONCEPTS

Fair Value Gap (FVG)

Meaning: Three-candle imbalance: candle 1 aur candle 3 ke beech non-overlap zone.

Chart par pehchanein: Bullish: candle 1 high se candle 3 low; bearish iska reverse.

Educational plan: FVG retest par reaction study karein; har gap fill nahi hota.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 04 · ICT CONCEPTS

Bullish FVG

Meaning: Strong upward displacement se bana bullish imbalance zone.

Chart par pehchanein: Candle 1 high aur candle 3 low ke beech gap mark karein.

Educational plan: Potential long only with context/trigger; SL structural invalidation ke neeche.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 05 · ICT CONCEPTS

Bearish FVG

Meaning: Strong downward displacement se bana bearish imbalance zone.

Chart par pehchanein: Candle 1 low aur candle 3 high ke beech gap mark karein.

Educational plan: Potential short only with context/trigger; SL structural invalidation ke upar.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 06 · ICT CONCEPTS

Inverse FVG (IFVG)

Meaning: Broken FVG opposite-side reaction zone ke roop mein study hota hai.

Chart par pehchanein: Price FVG ko decisively cross kare; retest par role reversal observe karein.

Educational plan: Confirmation ke bina zone flip assume na karein; stop invalidation ke paar.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 07 · ICT CONCEPTS

OTE — Optimal Trade Entry

Meaning: Common ICT Fibonacci retracement framework, often 62–79% band.

Chart par pehchanein: Meaningful impulse swing par fib anchor karein; confluence aur trigger ka wait.

Educational plan: OTE location filter hai, automatic entry nahi; SL swing invalidation ke paar.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 08 · ICT CONCEPTS

ICT Kill Zones

Meaning: London/New York ke selected time windows jahan activity badal sakti hai.

Chart par pehchanein: Broker/server time aur daylight-saving conversion verify karein; spread/news check.

Educational plan: Time window setup nahi banata; entry model aur risk rules zaroori.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 09 · ICT CONCEPTS

Judas Swing

Meaning: Session ke aas-paas initial false move ka narrative, phir opposite expansion hypothesis.

Chart par pehchanein: Range edge sweep, return inside aur displacement/structure confirmation dekhein.

Educational plan: False move assume karke counter-trend trade na karein; confirmation wait karein.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 10 · ICT CONCEPTS

ICT Entry Model

Meaning: Structured sequence: liquidity event, displacement/MSS, POI retracement, execution.

Chart par pehchanein: Higher timeframe context → sweep → MSS → FVG/OB retest.

Educational plan: Entry trigger, invalidation SL aur logical liquidity target pehle define karein.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 01 · SMC CONCEPTS

Break of Structure (BOS)

Meaning: Existing directional structure mein meaningful swing ka break.

Chart par pehchanein: Swing level mark karein; close, displacement aur context dekhein.

Educational plan: Continuation hypothesis; wick-only break ko confirm signal na samjhein.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 02 · SMC CONCEPTS

Change of Character (CHoCH)

Meaning: Structure ke behaviour mein pehla possible shift.

Chart par pehchanein: Prior trend ka relevant opposing swing break aur follow-through dekhein.

Educational plan: Early clue hai; higher timeframe confirmation helpful.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 03 · SMC CONCEPTS

Bullish Order Block

Meaning: Displacement se pehle last bearish candle/range ko potential demand POI ke roop mein mark karna.

Chart par pehchanein: Strong move aur meaningful structure break ke saath zone validate karein.

Educational plan: Retest confirmation; SL zone/structure invalidation ke neeche.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 04 · SMC CONCEPTS

Bearish Order Block

Meaning: Displacement se pehle last bullish candle/range ko potential supply POI ke roop mein mark karna.

Chart par pehchanein: Strong selloff aur relevant structure break ke context mein mark karein.

Educational plan: Retest confirmation; SL zone/structure invalidation ke upar.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 05 · SMC CONCEPTS

Breaker Block

Meaning: Failed OB ke decisively break hone ke baad opposite-side role reversal hypothesis.

Chart par pehchanein: OB break, acceptance aur retest/rejection observe karein.

Educational plan: Failed zone ko blindly trade na karein; new structure confirmation chahiye.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 06 · SMC CONCEPTS

Mitigation Block

Meaning: Prior opposing positioning unwind hone ka trader-defined zone concept.

Chart par pehchanein: Structure/liquidity context aur reaction area ko consistently mark karein.

Educational plan: Terminology vary karti hai; standalone signal nahi.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 07 · SMC CONCEPTS

Premium & Discount

Meaning: Selected dealing range ka upper half premium, lower half discount; midpoint equilibrium.

Chart par pehchanein: Meaningful swing high/low choose karein aur 50% mark karein.

Educational plan: Bullish discount/bearish premium context filter hai, entry trigger nahi.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 08 · SMC CONCEPTS

Supply & Demand

Meaning: Areas jahan se price ne strong departure dikhaya; potential reaction zones.

Chart par pehchanein: Base aur impulsive departure identify karein; zone boundaries consistent rakhein.

Educational plan: Zone retest guaranteed nahi; invalidation aur risk define karein.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 09 · SMC CONCEPTS

Displacement

Meaning: Strong directional repricing, wide candles aur relatively low overlap.

Chart par pehchanein: Move, candle closes, structure break aur possible imbalance assess karein.

Educational plan: Late chase na karein; retracement/confirmation ka wait.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 10 · SMC CONCEPTS

Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Meaning: Higher timeframe context ko lower timeframe execution se align karna.

Chart par pehchanein: Daily/4H levels → 1H/15M POI → 5M/1M trigger (tested workflow).

Educational plan: Timeframes ki conflicting stories se bachein; neutral/no-trade valid hai.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 01 · LIQUIDITY CONCEPTS

Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL)

Meaning: Obvious highs ke upar potential stop/breakout order area.

Chart par pehchanein: Equal highs, prior highs aur swing highs mark karein.

Educational plan: BSL target ya sweep context hai; reversal guaranteed nahi.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 02 · LIQUIDITY CONCEPTS

Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL)

Meaning: Obvious lows ke neeche potential stop/breakout order area.

Chart par pehchanein: Equal lows, prior lows aur swing lows mark karein.

Educational plan: SSL sweep ke baad bhi trend continue kar sakta hai.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 03 · LIQUIDITY CONCEPTS

Equal Highs (EQH)

Meaning: Similar highs jo traders ke liye visible liquidity reference ho sakte hain.

Chart par pehchanein: Repeated highs ko zone ke roop mein mark karein; exact equality required nahi.

Educational plan: Level ke upar acceptance vs return inside observe karein.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 04 · LIQUIDITY CONCEPTS

Equal Lows (EQL)

Meaning: Similar lows jo potential sell-side liquidity reference ho sakte hain.

Chart par pehchanein: Repeated lows/zone mark karein; instrument volatility consider karein.

Educational plan: Level touch par automatic buy/sell nahi.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 05 · LIQUIDITY CONCEPTS

Liquidity Sweep

Meaning: Price obvious level ke paar trade karke range ke andar return karta hai.

Chart par pehchanein: Wick/close back inside ke baad displacement aur MSS confirmation dekhein.

Educational plan: Wick alone entry nahi; stop sweep extreme ke beyond plan ho sakta hai.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 06 · LIQUIDITY CONCEPTS

Liquidity Grab

Meaning: Short-lived move through a visible level; usage traders ke beech vary karti hai.

Chart par pehchanein: Level breach, quick rejection aur follow-through observe karein.

Educational plan: Sweep/grab terminology ko apne rules mein clearly define karein.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 07 · LIQUIDITY CONCEPTS

Internal vs External Liquidity

Meaning: Range ke andar minor pivots internal; range boundaries ke beyond external references.

Chart par pehchanein: Dealing range define karein; internal swing points aur outer highs/lows mark karein.

Educational plan: Range arbitrary na banayein; higher-timeframe context use karein.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 08 · LIQUIDITY CONCEPTS

Inducement (IDM)

Meaning: Minor swing/setup jo early participants ko attract kar sakta hai—interpretive concept.

Chart par pehchanein: Main POI se pehle local pivot aur subsequent clearance observe karein.

Educational plan: Manipulation certainty claim na karein; objective rules rakhein.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 09 · LIQUIDITY CONCEPTS

Liquidity Run

Meaning: Price multiple visible liquidity levels ko trend direction mein clear karta hai.

Chart par pehchanein: Repeated level breaks, acceptance aur continuation structure dekhein.

Educational plan: Run ke against premature reversal trade se bachein.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal

LESSON 10 · LIQUIDITY CONCEPTS

Liquidity-Based Entry Model

Meaning: Liquidity event ko structure shift aur POI confirmation ke saath combine karna.

Chart par pehchanein: Key level → sweep/acceptance → displacement/MSS → retest.

Educational plan: Entry, technical SL, target, position size aur no-trade condition predefine karein.

Illustrative chart only · No live signal
