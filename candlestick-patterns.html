📖 Why Candlesticks Matter

Candlestick charts were invented by Japanese rice trader Homma Munehisa in the 1700s. He discovered that market prices are not just driven by supply and demand — but also by emotions of traders. Every candle tells you 4 crucial things:

O
Open
Where price started
H
High
Highest reached
L
Low
Lowest reached
C
Close
Where price ended

🔑 Key principle: A candlestick pattern is only meaningful when it appears at a key price level (support, resistance, or a major swing point). A Hammer in the middle of nowhere means nothing. A Hammer at a key support = high-probability trade.

🕯️ Anatomy of a Candlestick

Every candlestick has 3 parts: the Body, Upper Wick (Shadow), and Lower Wick. The body shows the range between Open and Close. Wicks show how far price went beyond the body but was rejected.

Green / Bullish
Close ABOVE Open. Buyers won the session.
Red / Bearish
Close BELOW Open. Sellers won the session.
Wick (Shadow)
Price went there but was rejected. Long wick = strong rejection.

Single Candlestick Patterns
Neutral / Reversal

Doji Family

A Doji forms when Open ≈ Close. It represents complete indecision — neither buyers nor sellers are in control. The type of Doji and its location on the chart determine its meaning. At key levels, Dojis are extremely powerful signals.

1. Standard Doji
Open = Close. Equal wicks above and below. Neither buyers nor sellers won. Perfect balance.
📍 Signal: Wait for next candle to confirm direction.
2. Dragonfly Doji 🐉
Open = Close = High. Very long lower wick. Buyers completely rejected the low prices and pushed price back to the top.
📍 Entry: BUY above next bullish candle. SL below the Doji low.
3. Gravestone Doji ⚰️
Open = Close = Low. Long upper wick. Sellers completely rejected the high prices and pushed back down to the bottom.
📍 Entry: SELL below next bearish candle. SL above Doji high.
4. Long-Legged Doji
Very long wicks both ways, tiny/no body. Wild session — both sides tried but failed. Market is confused.
📍 Signal: Major move coming. Watch for breakout.
5. Four Price Doji
Open = High = Low = Close. Just a flat horizontal line. Extremely rare. Seen in very low-volume markets or news lockdown periods.
📍 Signal: Complete market halt. Avoid trading.
Reversal Signals

Hammer Family

Hammer patterns have a small body and a long wick on one side. They signal strong rejection of a price level. The SAME shape can be bullish or bearish depending on where it appears in the trend.

6. Hammer 🔨
Small body at top. Long lower wick = 2x or 3x the body size. Buyers pushed price back up from a low. Found at the bottom of a downtrend.
📍 Entry: BUY above next green candle. SL = below hammer low.
7. Inverted Hammer
Small body at bottom. Long upper wick. Buyers tried to push higher. Appears at bottom of downtrend. Weaker than Hammer — needs confirmation.
📍 Entry: Wait for next green candle to confirm before buying.
8. Hanging Man 🪝
Same shape as Hammer but appears at top of uptrend. Sellers tested lower prices. Weakness is showing — trend may reverse down.
📍 Entry: SELL below next red candle. SL above Hanging Man high.
9. Shooting Star ⭐
Small body at bottom. Long upper wick = 2x+ body. At top of uptrend. Sellers strongly rejected higher prices. One of the most reliable single-candle bearish signals.
📍 Entry: SELL on next red candle. SL above Shooting Star high.
Momentum

Marubozu

Marubozu means "bald" in Japanese — no wicks at all. This is the most powerful momentum candle. One side dominated completely from open to close with zero hesitation.

10. Bullish Marubozu

No wicks. Opens at Low, Closes at High. 100% buyers controlled the session. Strong momentum — more upside expected.

Body itself becomes key support zone. Buy pullbacks to this body.
11. Bearish Marubozu

No wicks. Opens at High, Closes at Low. 100% sellers controlled. Strong bearish momentum — more downside expected.

Body becomes key resistance. Sell any rally into this body zone.
Indecision & Momentum

Spinning Top & Belt Hold

12. Spinning Top (Bullish)
Small body, equal wicks both sides. Both buyers and sellers active but neither dominates. Market is hesitating.
At support = bullish reversal possible.
13. Spinning Top (Bearish)
Same as bullish version but a red body. At resistance levels this signals a potential reversal downward.
At resistance = bearish reversal possible.
14. Bullish Belt Hold
Opens at the low of the session (no lower wick), closes near the high. Strong bullish candle. Buyers controlled from the very open.
📍 Bullish signal. Enter BUY on next candle.
15. Bearish Belt Hold
Opens at the high of the session (no upper wick), closes near the low. Sellers controlled from the very open. Strong bearish signal.
📍 Bearish signal. Enter SELL on next candle.
Double Candlestick Patterns
Strong Reversal

Engulfing Patterns

One of the most reliable and widely-used reversal patterns. The second candle completely engulfs (covers) the entire body of the first candle — showing a complete shift in who is in control.

16. Bullish Engulfing 🟢

At the bottom of a downtrend: green candle's body fully covers the previous red candle's body. Buyers took complete control. Very high probability reversal.

✅ Entry: BUY on close of green candle
🛑 SL: Below the low of the pattern
🎯 TP: Next resistance (min 1:2 RR)
17. Bearish Engulfing 🔴

At the top of an uptrend: red candle's body fully covers the previous green candle's body. Sellers took complete control. High probability sell signal.

✅ Entry: SELL on close of red candle
🛑 SL: Above the high of the pattern
🎯 TP: Next support (min 1:2 RR)
Reversal (Needs Confirmation)

Harami Patterns

"Harami" means pregnant in Japanese. The first candle is large, the second is small and fits inside the first candle's body like a baby. It signals momentum is fading but needs confirmation.

18. Bullish Harami
Large red candle, then small green inside it. Bearish momentum slowing. At support = possible bottom. Needs next candle to confirm.
Wait for 3rd green candle. Then BUY.
19. Bearish Harami
Large green candle, then small red inside it. Bullish momentum slowing. At resistance = possible top. Needs next candle to confirm.
Wait for 3rd red candle. Then SELL.
20. Bullish Harami Cross
Same as Bullish Harami but the second candle is a Doji. Stronger signal because the Doji shows complete indecision after the strong sell.
Stronger bullish reversal than regular Harami.
21. Bearish Harami Cross
Same as Bearish Harami but the second candle is a Doji. Stronger bearish signal because Doji shows complete indecision after the strong buy.
Stronger bearish reversal than regular Harami.
Precision Reversal

Tweezer Patterns

Two candles with the exactly same High (top) or same Low (bottom). Price tested a level twice and was rejected both times. Institutions are defending that exact price.

22. Tweezer Bottom 📌

Both candles reach the same low. Price tested that level twice and was rejected. Strong buyers at that exact price. BUY above 2nd candle. SL below twin lows.

23. Tweezer Top 📌

Both candles reach the same high. Price tried to break higher twice but was rejected. Strong sellers defending that level. SELL below 2nd candle. SL above twin highs.

Moderate Reversal

Piercing Line & Dark Cloud Cover

24. Piercing Line

Large red candle. Then green candle opens BELOW the red's low but closes ABOVE 50% of the red body. Buyers fighting back. BUY on confirmation.

25. Dark Cloud Cover

Large green candle. Then red candle opens ABOVE the green's high but closes BELOW 50% of the green body. Sellers fighting back. SELL on confirmation.

Most Powerful 2-Candle

Kicker Patterns

The Kicker is the most powerful 2-candle pattern. Price gaps completely in the opposite direction from one candle to the next. This shows an instantaneous, violent shift in market sentiment — usually driven by major news.

26. Bullish Kicker 🚀

A red candle, then price gaps UP and opens as a bullish (green) candle. The gap is never retested. Extremely bullish signal. Often appears after news.

27. Bearish Kicker 📉

A green candle, then price gaps DOWN and opens as a bearish (red) candle. The gap is never retested. Extremely bearish signal. One of the strongest sells.

Reversal

Meeting Lines (Counterattack)

Two candles where the second candle closes at the same price as the first. After an opposite-colored large candle, the second candle reverses and closes at the same level.

28. Bullish Meeting Lines

After a large red candle, the green candle opens lower but closes at the SAME price as the red candle. Buyers absorbed all the selling pressure. Bullish reversal signal.

29. Bearish Meeting Lines

After a large green candle, the red candle opens higher but closes at the SAME price as the green candle. Sellers absorbed all the buying pressure. Bearish reversal signal.

Triple Candlestick Patterns
Most Powerful Reversal

Star Patterns

Star patterns are 3-candle formations and among the most reliable reversal signals in all of technical analysis. Widely trusted by institutional traders globally.

30. Morning Star ⭐ (Bullish)
1

Large bearish candle — sellers in full control

2

Small candle (star) — indecision, sellers losing power

3

Large bullish candle closing above midpoint of candle 1

✅ Entry: BUY on close of candle 3
🛑 SL: Below candle 2 low
🎯 TP: Previous resistance (min 1:3 RR)
31. Evening Star 🌙 (Bearish)
1

Large bullish candle — buyers in full control

2

Small candle at top — indecision, buyers exhausted

3

Large bearish candle closing below midpoint of candle 1

✅ Entry: SELL on close of candle 3
🛑 SL: Above candle 2 high
🎯 TP: Previous support (min 1:3 RR)
32. Morning Doji Star ⭐ (Stronger)

Same as Morning Star but the middle candle is a Doji (perfect indecision). The reversal signal is significantly stronger because the Doji shows sellers completely lost control before buyers took over.

Stronger than regular Morning Star. Enter BUY on candle 3 close.
33. Evening Doji Star 🌙 (Stronger)

Same as Evening Star but the middle candle is a Doji (perfect indecision). Much stronger bearish reversal because the Doji shows buyers completely lost control before sellers took over.

Stronger than regular Evening Star. Enter SELL on candle 3 close.
Trend Continuation

Three Soldiers & Three Crows

34. Three White Soldiers 🏹

3 consecutive large green candles, each closing higher than the previous and each opening within the prior body. Strong uptrend continuation. Don't chase — buy the pullback.

35. Three Black Crows 🦅

3 consecutive large red candles, each closing lower. Strong downtrend signal. Appears after a top or during a breakdown. Sell any rally. Bearish momentum is very strong.

Confirmed Reversal

Three Inside & Three Outside

These are Harami and Engulfing patterns confirmed by a 3rd candle. Much more reliable because the reversal is proven — not just potential.

36. Three Inside Up
Red → Small Green inside → Big Green breaking above. Confirmed bullish reversal. Enter BUY on 3rd candle close.
37. Three Inside Down
Green → Small Red inside → Big Red breaking below. Confirmed bearish reversal. Enter SELL on 3rd candle close.
38. Three Outside Up

Bullish Engulfing (2 candles) then a 3rd candle that closes even higher = confirmed uptrend reversal. Most reliable of the three-candle bullish patterns.

Engulfing + Confirm candle = high probability BUY.
39. Three Outside Down

Bearish Engulfing (2 candles) then a 3rd candle that closes even lower = confirmed downtrend reversal. Most reliable of the three-candle bearish patterns.

Engulfing + Confirm candle = high probability SELL.
Rare & Extremely Powerful

Abandoned Baby

One of the rarest and most powerful candlestick patterns. A Doji appears isolated between two large candles with gaps on both sides. When you see this, act immediately — it almost never fails at key levels.

40. Bullish Abandoned Baby 👶

Large red candle → gap down → isolated Doji → gap up → large green candle. Both gaps show the Doji is completely isolated. Extremely strong buy signal.

✅ Entry: BUY on close of green candle
🛑 SL: Below the Doji low
🎯 Min R:R: 1:4
41. Bearish Abandoned Baby 👶

Large green candle → gap up → isolated Doji → gap down → large red candle. Both gaps show the Doji is completely isolated. Extremely strong sell signal.

✅ Entry: SELL on close of red candle
🛑 SL: Above the Doji high
🎯 Min R:R: 1:4
Rare & Advanced Patterns
Advanced

Rare & Advanced Patterns

These patterns are less common but appear in real markets. Knowing them gives you a significant edge over traders who only know the basics. Most traders have never heard of these.

42. Three Line Strike (Bullish)

3 consecutive red candles followed by 1 large green candle that engulfs all 3. Counter-intuitive: this is actually a bullish continuation. Bears exhausted, bulls take over.

BUY after 4th green candle closes. Very reliable.
43. Three Line Strike (Bearish)

3 consecutive red candles followed by 1 large green candle that engulfs all 3. Looks bullish but is actually a bearish continuation signal. Institutions buy back briefly to sell more at higher prices.

Counter-intuitive: SELL the rally after the 4th green candle.
44. Upside Gap Two Crows

Large green candle, then 2 small red candles that gap up but stay above the green candle. Bearish reversal forming at the top. The gap is the warning sign — sellers appearing despite high prices.

Bearish reversal. Watch for breakdown below the gap.
45. Stick Sandwich

Red candle, then green candle, then red candle with the SAME closing price as the first red candle. The green is sandwiched. A bullish reversal signal — sellers tried twice at the same level and couldn't break lower.

Bullish reversal. Double-bottom forming at same close price.
46. Advance Block

Three green candles like White Soldiers but each has progressively smaller bodies and longer upper wicks. Shows buyers are weakening and losing momentum. Bearish reversal warning — uptrend about to stall.

Warning sign at resistance. Reduce longs, watch for reversal.
47. High Wave Candle

Small body with extremely long wicks on BOTH sides. Huge volatility with no clear direction. Price traveled far up AND down but closed near the open. Market is in maximum confusion.

Avoid trading. Wait for clarity. Major move incoming.
⚡ Entry Module

How to Trade Candlestick Patterns

A pattern alone means nothing. It's the location + pattern + confirmation that creates a high-probability trade. Use this 3-step system for every trade:

1

Location

Is the pattern forming at a key support, resistance, or major swing level? Location is 60% of the trade quality. A pattern in the middle of nowhere is worthless.

2

Pattern

Is there a valid candlestick pattern? Must show clear rejection or reversal energy at the key level. The candle must be complete — never enter on an unfinished candle.

3

Confirmation

Wait for the next candle to confirm the direction. Enter on close of confirmation candle. Never FOMO — if you miss it, the market will give you another setup.
