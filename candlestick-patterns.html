🕯️ Module 2: Price Behavior

Mastering Candlestick Patterns

Sikhein ki candlestick kya hoti hai, yeh market me buyers aur sellers ka mood kaise batati hai, aur charts par inka use karke accurate trade entry kaise li jaati hai.

Candlestick Pattern Kya Hai aur Iska Market Me Kya Role Hai?

Ek candlestick chart par price ke utaar-chadhaw (movement) ko dikhane ka sabse behtareen tarika hai. Iske zariye hamein ek hi nazar me 4 cheezein pata chalti hain: Open (Shuruat), High (Sabse Upar), Low (Sabse Neeche), aur Close (Ant).

Market Me Role: Candlestick sirf line nahi hai, yeh market ke psychology (psychology of traders) ko darshati hai. Jab bade institutional players ya smart money market me entry karte hain, toh candles ka size aur unki wick (poonch) bata deti hai ki ab market kis disha me jaane wala hai.

Sabse Important Candlestick Patterns (Charts me Kaise Pehchanein)

1. Hammer Pattern (Hathoda Candle)

Trend Reversal Sign (Bullish)

Long Lower Wick

Yeh Kya Hai: Iska chota sa body upar hota hai aur lambi poonch (wick) neeche ki taraf hoti hai (bilkul hathode jaisi).

Market Me Kaise Kaam Karta Hai: Jab market gir raha hota hai aur support level par achanak Hammer candle banti hai, toh iska matlab hai ki sellers ne price ko neeche dhakela tha lekin buyers ne zor lagakar price ko wapas upar khinch liya.

Chart Par Kaise Dekhein: Downtrend ke ant me support ke paas Hammer dikhe toh **BUY** trade ka strong signal mana jata hai.

2. Shooting Star Pattern

Trend Reversal Sign (Bearish)

Long Upper Wick

Yeh Kya Hai: Yeh Hammer ka bilkul ulta hota hai. Iski lambi wick upar ki taraf hoti hai aur chota body neeche hota hai.

Market Me Kaise Kaam Karta Hai: Jab market badh raha hota hai aur resistance level par Shooting Star banti hai, iska matlab buyers ne price upar le jaane ki koshish ki par sellers ne haavi hokar price ko wapas neeche gira diya.

Chart Par Kaise Dekhein: Uptrend ke top par resistance ke paas yeh dikhe toh **SELL** ka sign hota hai.

3. Doji Pattern (Plus Sign Candle)

Market Indecision (Confusion)

Equal Open & Close

Yeh Kya Hai: Doji candle wahi khulti hai aur wahi band ho jaati hai (plus ya cross (+) jaisi dikhti hai).

Market Me Kaise Kaam Karta Hai: Iska matlab hai ki na toh buyers jeet paaye aur na hi sellers. Market me poori tarah confusion (indecision) hai.

Chart Par Kaise Dekhein: Agar lamba trend chal raha hai aur achanak Doji ban jaye, toh samajh jao ki ab trend rukne wala hai ya reverse hone wala hai.

4. Engulfing Pattern (Bullish & Bearish)

Strong Momentum Takeover

→
Badi Candle Choti ko Nigal Leti Hai

Yeh Kya Hai: Jab doosri candle pehli candle ko poori tarah se apne andar cover (engulf) kar leti hai.

Market Me Kaise Kaam Karta Hai:
• Bullish Engulfing: Red candle ke baad ek badi green candle bane jo red ko poori tarah dhak le (Strong Buy).
• Bearish Engulfing: Green candle ke baad ek badi red candle bane jo green ko dhak le (Strong Sell).

Chart Par Kaise Dekhein: Support ya Resistance ke paas Engulfing pattern sabse zyada accurate result deta hai.
